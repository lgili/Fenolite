# Changelog

All notable changes to Fenolite are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) (pre-1.0: minor versions may break).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- v0.1 acceptance: a second example board of forty parts, the loop proved on both examples and both KiCad majors, the agent guide `agent/SKILL.md`, the `wheel` and `dco` jobs, Windows and Python 3.11 to 3.13 in CI, and the release record `docs/release/v0.1.md`.
- Board analyses: `fenolite analyze PATH` measures the current capacity of tracks, arcs and vias from one published fit (each constant recorded with its source in `docs/analyses.md`; `decimal` arithmetic, rounded down), the clearance of pairs of nets through air, and their creepage along the board surface, around cut-outs and around the board edge (`fenolite.analysis`). Fenolite measures and the user decides: requirements come from a user TOML file (`fenolite.requirements.v0`, integers only, a voltage table looked up without interpolation), no requirement value and no default thickness or temperature rise is shipped, and every reply is `INFERRED` or lower. Ten `analysis.*` codes; an error finding exits 5. A recorded probe on `kicad-cli` 10.0.6 shows that KiCad's `creepage` rule agrees with Fenolite around a slot (11 mm) and reports nothing for copper on opposite faces across the board edge.
- DSL footprint authoring: a design-scoped builder creates pads and graphics; exact-ID assignments resolve in KiCad and experimental Altium builds (c0055).
- DSL component pin-to-pad mapping and authored slotted drills; KiCad writes supported aligned oval drills, while Altium reports its unsupported slot geometry (c0056).
- Routing plugins: a router protocol with an entry-point registry, `fenolite route` with the built-in `direct` router and the KiCadRoutingTools plugin (an external MIT tool run as a subprocess at a pinned tag), routed copper merged into the board and kept by later builds (c0016).
- Design rules in the script: `design.rules.minimum(clearance=…, track_width=…, via_diameter=…, via_drill=…, hole_size=…, edge_clearance=…, netclass=None)` declares design-rule minimums for the board or for one net class. They reach `rules.json`, the custom rules of `<name>.kicad_dru` and the board-setup minimums of `<name>.kicad_pro`, and `kicad-cli pcb drc` 9.0.9 and 10.0.6 enforce them (`H-K-DSL-MINIMUM`). The Altium build does not write them and reports them with `altium.not-lowered` (c0054).
- Freerouting plugin (c0023): `fenolite route --router freerouting` routes a board with Freerouting 2.4.1, a GPL-3.0 autorouter run as a subprocess with its analytics disabled (`FENOLITE_FREEROUTING_JAR` or `--router-path`, Java 25, `--router-option max-passes=N`; `docker:<image>` runs a container without a network). Fenolite writes the Specctra design file itself and reads the session back (`fenolite.backends.specctra`: `dsn.write_dsn`, `ses.read_session`, `ses.to_copper`, a codec and not a registered backend): one image per footprint, board-frame padstacks, nets and classes, keep-outs, existing copper as protected wiring. `RoutingJob.extra` carries the board-frame pads and the outline to plugins; `doctor` reports the jar and its Java; `route.option-ignored` is a new issue code. The router is listed with `sends_data_offsite: true` and needs `--allow-offsite` until its run without a network is recorded. ADR-0006 (`Proposed`) covers the facts taken from the Specctra reference.
- Zone fill: `fenolite fill` refills a board through `kicad-cli` 10 on a copy and writes the fills into the board for its own KiCad major, target 9 included; `check` gains the `zone.fill` stage; `--kicad-cli docker:<image>` runs `kicad-cli` in a container (c0015).
- Placement: `fenolite place` puts staged parts on the board in a deterministic grid or moves named parts, a legality check reports courtyard overlaps and parts outside the outline in `build` and `place`, and a placed part is kept by later builds. `fenolite place PATH` (`--strategy grid|manual`, `--move REF=X,Y[,ROT[,SIDE]]`, `--only`, `--pitch`, `--gap`, `--margin`, `--force`, `-o`) writes only with `--confirm` and refuses an illegal placement without `--force`; `fenolite.placement` holds `check` (exact courtyard overlap, outside-outline and edge-clearance tests on `PlacedExtent`s) and the grid placer; `backends.kicad.replace.move_footprint` translates a footprint or re-places it from its library definition with its uuid, fields, lock and pad nets kept; `backends.kicad.outline.board_outline` gives the outline as rings from the model or from `Edge.Cuts`; `fenolite build` reports `place.*` issues as warnings (`result.placement`) and never refuses for placement. Probes on `kicad-cli` 9.0.9 and 10.0.6: moved footprints read back at the requested position, rotation and side with their pad nets, and courtyards that only touch give no `courtyards_overlap`. `H-K-PLACE-MOVE` and `H-K-PLACE-TOUCH` are `KICAD-VERIFIED (9.0.x, 10.0.x)`, so a `place` reply carries `evidence.level` `KICAD-VERIFIED`; the outline rings stay `INFERRED` (`H-G-PLACE-OUTLINE`: 3 of 21 demo boards have an outline in KiCad and no ring here). KiCad's DRC judges the board (change c0022).
- Footprint fields: Reference, Value and every placed property of a footprint are typed fields (position, angle, layer, visibility, size, thickness, justification, mirror), read from 8.0 to 10.0 boards and written for 9.0 and 10.0; `Part.field(...)` and `fields(design)` place Reference and Value from the DSL, also outside the courtyard on a chosen side; rebuilds keep fields edited in KiCad unless the script locks them (`result.preserved.fields`); checked by `kicad-cli` 9.0 and 10.0 (`fenolite.model.FootprintField`, `fenolite.backends.kicad.fields`, `fenolite.lens.fields`; c0030).
- Zone settings: zones carry typed settings (clearance, minimum thickness, pad connection solid/thermal/none/thru-hole-only, thermal gap and spoke width, island removal, smoothing, hatched fill), read from KiCad 9.0 and 10.0 boards and written per target; pads carry a zone connection override; `Design.zone()` declares pours in design scripts, and rebuilds keep zones edited in KiCad unless the script locks them; created target-9 zones write `(filled_areas_thickness no)`, so their fills are plotted as written; checked by refills on `kicad-cli` 10.0 and loads on 9.0 (c0031).
- Copper check: Fenolite now judges shorts and clearance of a board's copper itself, exactly and without `kicad-cli`. `fenolite.geometry.thick` gives thick shapes (a core swept by a disc) with exact touch, closer-than, gap and witness tests; `fenolite.checks.check_copper` judges tracks, arcs, vias, board-frame pads and zone fills pair by pair, with the clearance in force taken from the project's own rules, net classes and board minimum (`checks.clearance`, `backends.base.DesignRules` and `DesignRulesSource`, `backends.kicad.copperrules`); `fenolite check` gains the default stage `copper.clearance`, between `erc.lite` and `drc.kicad`, and six codes (`copper.short`, `copper.clearance`, `copper.zone-overlap`, `copper.rules-incomplete`, `copper.item-unsupported`, `copper.clearance-unset`); `fenolite build` judges the triad it is about to write and refuses a short or a clearance error (`--copper-check refuse`, the default) or reports it and writes (`--copper-check warn`), with `result.copper_check`. Probes on `kicad-cli` 9.0.9 and 10.0.6 show why: a via that touches a track of another net whose net owns a pad is loaded on that net and no short is reported, copper without pads is merged into one net, and 10.0.6 reports a short between two nets with pads in about half of its runs; the verdicts of `check_copper` and of KiCad's DRC agree on every parity canary (seven pair kinds under a rule, a class and a board-minimum clearance, six resolution cases, and zone fills on 10.0.6). A board that holds a real short now exits 5 under `check` (`docs/formats/kicad/copper.md`, `docs/evidence/copper-check.md`, change c0029).
- Board-frame queries and manual copper: `board_pads`/`find_pads` give every pad's board-frame position, rotation, layers, net, hole and exact copper entries (all pads sharing a number); `placed_extent` gives a placed footprint's front and back courtyard with rotation and side applied; `BoardFrame` in `backends.base`; DSL copper intents (`Part.pad`, `via_step`, `Design.track`, `Design.via`, `Design.stitch`) resolved by the build after placement, with nets inferred and checked, version-8 uuids from the caller's key, and stale script copper removed on rebuild; checked by `kicad-cli` 9.0 and 10.0. `fenolite build` reports `result.copper`; the routed example is `examples/blink_routed`, and the guide is `docs/copper.md` (c0028).
- Altium change order without class differences (change c0048): a net class is declared in the schematic by a Parameter Set directive with a `ClassName` parameter on each of its nets, once per sheet, and a new project file ends with `[PrjClassGen]` (`NetClassManualEnabled=1`), so "Design » Update PCB Document" no longer proposes to remove the class from the board; the PCB document holds one component class per schematic sheet (named after the module, or after the sheet for the top sheet and for the single sheet of a flat build), and a project file with a PCB document or module sheets turns the rooms off for every schematic document. "Supply Nets" rules and rooms are not written and are documented as differences Altium may still propose (`docs/altium.md`, "Change order"). The golden schematics and project files of the samples with a net class or module sheets are rebuilt. Experimental; the maintainer's report of 2026-10-04 (AD 26.5) found only the "Supply Nets" rules in the change order of a build with module sheets, and the repeat of the same day on the rebuilt samples found the same for a flat build (`H-A-ECO-SHEETCLASS`; Part E of `docs/evidence/altium-pcb.md`).
- Altium PCB copper: the experimental `<name>.PcbDoc` now holds tracks, arcs and through vias, zones as unpoured polygon pours (repour in Altium), a 2- or 4-layer stack whose inner layers are signal layers or internal planes (`design.board(..., copper=4, planes={...})`), net classes and Clearance, Width and Routing Via Style rules; `--copper-from board.kicad_pcb` copies the copper of a routed KiCad board after checking that it matches the design, and the board's placements win; blind, buried and micro vias, split planes and copper outside the stack are refused; checked by `kicad-cli pcb import`; evidence `INFERRED`, with the maintainer's Altium Designer report recorded as an author report (c0038).
- KiCad libraries: pinned fetch and verified cache of the official libraries at 10.0.6 and 9.0.9 (`tools/kicad_libs_fetch.py`, `FENOLITE_LIBS_CACHE`), a `cache` library source, opt-in `kicad_common.json` path variables (`LibraryConfig.read_common`), a directory-scan fallback (row origin `scan`), builds that resolve from the cache and report `scan` rows, a census of the fetched trees (the 10.0.6 install equals its pinned tag), demo-library corpus rows (`libs`), an `env` keyword on `KicadCli.drc`, and `kicad-cli` probes for relative paths, the versioned fallback, nested tables, the configuration folder and KiCad's own path variables. The probes refuted one rule: KiCad resolves a relative library `uri` against its working directory, not against the table's folder, so the resolver now joins a relative `uri` to the project folder for rows of every table (a nested table's `../x` now means the folder above the project, not above the nested table).
- No-connect pins: `fenolite.dsl.no_connect(*pins)` marks pins as intentionally unconnected; the model keeps them as `Circuit.no_connects` (additive, schema regenerated); the Altium schematic gets a No ERC directive (record 22) at each marked pin, in both forms; the KiCad build keeps the marks in `.fenolite/` and refuses a marked pin on a net (`build.no-connect-on-net`, `model.no-connect-on-net`); `erc.lite.floating-pin` skips marked pins; evidence `INFERRED`, with the maintainer's Altium Designer report recorded as an author report (pending: Part N of `docs/evidence/altium-schematic.md`).
- Altium hierarchy and harnesses: `fenolite build --target altium --altium-sheets modules` writes a top sheet with sheet symbols and one sheet per top-level module, with matching ports and sheet entries; `fenolite.dsl.Harness(name, members)` groups nets, drawn as harness connectors, entries and signal harness lines in the binary schematic, with `.Harness` definition files; the PCB document links parts on module sheets through their sheet symbol; the default `flat` output is unchanged; evidence `INFERRED`, with the maintainer's Altium Designer report recorded as an author report (c0037). After the maintainer's report of 2026-10-03 (AD 26.5): a harness between two neighbouring modules is drawn on the top sheet as one signal harness line between the two sheet entries, without connector blocks and labels, which removes the "multiple names" warnings; the top sheet then gets no `.Harness` file; a new project file of a `modules` build lists the schematic documents first and together (the top sheet, the module sheets, then the PCB document, the libraries and the harness files), because with the PCB document and the libraries in between Altium Designer took only the first module sheet into the hierarchy (`H-A-SCH-HIER-ORDER`, confirmed by the maintainer's second report; the earlier guess about small sheets, `H-A-SCHBIN-MINI`, is refuted and its padding parameter `FenoliteNote` is removed); a flat build keeps its project file bytes, and an existing project file is kept as it is; `CHANNELOFFSET` of a PCB component counts the parts of its own sheet; a readback test checks that every board component links to a schematic component through a sheet symbol and that every module sheet is reachable from the top sheet.
- `fenolite export` and `fenolite render`: fabrication files (Gerbers and the job file, drill, positions, IPC-D-356) and review views (SVG plots, rendered PNG) through `kicad-cli` on a copy of the project, written only with `--confirm`, with the manifest `fenolite-artifacts.json` (`schemas/fenolite.artifacts.v0.json`): per file its SHA-256 and a date-stripped `content_sha256`, because KiCad stamps the creation date into Gerber and drill files. When one kind fails nothing is written. Opt-in `render` stage in `check` (`--stages render`), which never gives an error. Commands may declare `example_tools`, and the suites run such examples against a fake `kicad-cli`. Measured on `kicad-cli` 9.0.9 and 10.0.6 (`docs/evidence/kicad-export.md`); user guide `docs/exports.md` (c0024).
- Altium target writes PCB files: `fenolite build --target altium` writes `<name>.PcbLib` with the resolved KiCad footprints (SMD and through-hole pads, roundrect corners, silkscreen, fabrication and courtyard lines and arcs) and, experimentally, `<name>.PcbDoc` with the outline, two-layer stack, placed components, pads, nets and designators, linked to the schematic by unique ids; empty streams in compound files; write kinds `altium_pcblib` and `altium_pcbdoc`; new `altium.*` issue codes; kicad-cli `fp upgrade` and `pcb import` as oracles; capabilities entry `altium-pcb-writer`; evidence `INFERRED`, Altium checks as author reports.
- `fenolite check` judges KiCad DRC violations as located `kicad.drc.<type>` issues, compares the pad nets of model, board and IPC-D-356 export, and runs RT2 on request; RT0 to RT2 pass on the corpus with kicad-cli 10.0.6 and on the 9.0.9.1 demo boards in the `kicad-9` job. Findings carry KiCad's severity (which follows the project's `rule_severities`) and are located as `REF-PIN`, `REF`, a board locator or the report position; an unrouted board now exits 5. `netlist.assignment_compare` (default stage) compares partitions of `REF-PIN` elements and locates a reassigned pad; `roundtrip.rt2` (opt-in with `--stages`) fails only where KiCad repeats its own DRC report and otherwise says that RT2 is not judged, because on boards with hundreds of violations two DRC runs of one file differ. New: `checks.{drc_json,assignment_compare,rt2}`, `backends.kicad.padnets`, `KicadOracle.netlist` and `.rt2`, `NetlistOracle`, `RoundTripOracle`, `PadNetList`, `NetlistOutcome` and `Rt2Outcome` in `backends.base`, `DrcReport.entries()`, the `rt2-9` corpus rows, and four recorded measurements (token census, 10.0 byte identity, 5 MiB throughput, Edge.Cuts chaining: one demo outline is open by 33 nm) (`docs/evidence/kicad-rt2.md`, `docs/cli-contract.md`).
- Altium target writes schematic libraries: `fenolite build --target altium` writes `<name>.SchLib` with the resolved KiCad symbols (pins, units, Part Zero, synthesised body) and a generic `X.SchLib` for each Altium link library, lists them in the project file and draws the schematic from the same geometry; `cfb.Storage` for nested storages; write kind `altium_schlib`; new `altium.*` issue codes; kicad-cli `sym upgrade` round trip as oracle; evidence `INFERRED`, Altium checks as author reports (c0034).
- Layout preservation: `fenolite build` over an existing project keeps footprints moved in KiCad (matched by uuid, `fenolite.path` or a `moved(old, new)` alias, with the precedence locked `place()` > board > `place()` > staging), routing and zones on nets that still exist, fills while their digests are unchanged (`zone.fill-stale` otherwise), board-only footprints with their pins on their pads' nets, board settings, project keys and user custom rules after Fenolite's; removed parts give `layout.orphan`; `fenolite check` no longer asks footprints added in KiCad for a symbol; rebuilds and a regenerated `.fenolite/` are byte-identical; `build.layout-exists` now guards only `fp-lib-table` and vendored footprints; checked by `kicad-cli` 9.0 and 10.0 (`fenolite.lens.preserve`, `Design.moved`, `dsl.moves`, `result.preserved`, `docs/lens.md`).
- Altium target writes the binary schematic by default: `fenolite build --target altium` writes `<name>.SchDoc` as an MS-CFB compound file (stdlib writer; `FileHeader` and `Storage` streams; deterministic bytes); `--altium-format ascii` keeps the ASCII form; write kind `altium_schdoc_binary`; issue `altium.schematic-too-large`; evidence `INFERRED`, with the maintainer's Altium 365 Viewer and Altium Designer checks recorded as author reports.

### Fixed

- Windows: `kicad-cli.exe` is found on `PATH` and in KiCad's default install folders, which KiCad's installer does not put on `PATH`; files Fenolite writes keep LF line ends; a board on another drive than the working folder can be routed; a drawing-sheet path starting with `/` counts as absolute.
- `fenolite route` reads the net classes from the project file beside the board. Before, every net was routed with the default track width, clearance and via size, whatever its class said: a KiCad board holds no net class.
- `fenolite route --router freerouting` lists a net under `unrouted` when Freerouting reports that it left one of its connections open. Before, a net with any copper counted as routed, so a partly routed net was reported as done; KiCad's DRC in `fenolite check` still found it.
- A board whose rotated pad or rotated footprint carries a slotted hole is read and written again, and its footprints can be written as library files: the writer refused them (`KiCad writer cannot represent this slot rotation`, `KiCad oval drills can encode only horizontal or vertical slot axes`). `Padstack.hole_rotation` is now the slot's axis in the pad's own frame everywhere; on a board it used to include the pad's angle.
- Geometry: the documented and tested bound of two successive `Transform.apply` calls is `(1 + |cos θ| + |sin θ|)/2` nm per axis, at most `(1 + √2)/2 ≈ 1.2071` nm, not 1 nm; `test_composition_rounds_once` no longer fails at random, and a test reaches the bound exactly. No code changes (c0050).
- Script copper in the Altium build (change c0053): `fenolite build --target altium` now writes the tracks, vias and stitching vias that the design script declares (`Design.track`, `Design.via`, `Design.stitch`) into the experimental `<name>.PcbDoc`; before, they were left out without an issue. An intent that does not resolve refuses the build, an intent that creates nothing is reported, and with `--copper-from` the board wins with one `altium.not-lowered` info.
- `fenolite check` no longer reports `kicad.drc.rules-not-loaded` for rules that were loaded on a board with 499 or more clearance violations: `kicad-cli` stops reporting clearance violations near 499 per run and can leave the canary's own out, so such a run is now `inconclusive` with the reason `clearance-limit` (`kicad.drc.rules-unchecked`). The two-run canary test compares two DRC runs against what was measured to repeat, and the contract says which part of `check`'s output repeats between runs (c0051).
- Altium PCB document in the form Altium saves: `<name>.PcbDoc` now holds the whole `Board6` record (board keys, the outline as a polygon, sheet, sub-stack, drill pair, routing keys and snap grid, written by rule in `docboard.py` on top of `libboard.py`), net and component records with the common keys and unique ids, `UniqueIDPrimitiveInformation`, the option storages (`Advanced Placer Options6`, `Pin Swap Options6`, `Design Rule Checker Options6`, `PadViaLibrary`, `PadViaLibraryCache`, `LayerKindMapping`, `ConstraintManager`, `SignalClasses`) and 13 more empty storages; `write_pcbdoc` takes the file name. The first form gave a "catastrophic" error in Altium Designer 26.5 (maintainer's report, c0035); the new one opens there and its change order from the schematic reports no difference (author report of 2026-10-03). Texts, pads, tracks and arcs keep their short records.
- Altium PCB library opens in Altium Designer's form: `<name>.PcbLib` now holds the 53-byte `FileHeader` (version and library id), the whole board record in `Library/Data` (layer stack, grid, layer sets and view keys, written by rule in `libboard.py`) and the `Library` side streams (`EmbeddedFonts`, `ModelsNoEmbed`, `Textures`, `ComponentParamsTOC`, `PadViaLibrary`); `FileHeaderSix` of `<name>.PcbDoc` carries its id. The first form gave a "catastrophic" error in Altium Designer (maintainer's report, c0035); the new one opens in Altium Designer 26.5 (author report of 2026-10-03).
