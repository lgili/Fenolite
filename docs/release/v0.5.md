# Release record: v0.5

What the release `0.5.0` ships, change by change: what each change ships, the test that proves it, in
which CI job, which of its tasks are still open and why. `0.5.0` is v0.5a (conversion between KiCad and
Altium with a report of what is kept or lost, the controlled KiCad downgrade and the public `equivalent`:
c0158 to c0162, with the correction c0163), the changes written on `dev` after `0.4.0` (c0155, c0156,
c0157, the two changes that hold the id c0164, c0165, c0166, and the graduation rule of c0092), the four
slot changes (c0167 and c0169, placed in `0.5.0` on 2026-10-15; c0168 and c0170, placed in it on
2026-10-16), and the two changes that the maintainer added on 2026-10-16: c0172 (native Altium Draftsman
documents) and c0173 (board panels). A row says `ships`, `ships with deferred tasks` or `pending`. The
verdict at the end is the maintainer's. `tests/unit/test_release_record_v05.py` keeps this page honest
about what exists.

**The maintainer's approval of 2026-10-15.** He approved preparing the release `0.5.0`; its publication
waits for his explicit yes. By his decision of the same day c0167 and c0169 are in `0.5.0`. The rule of his
decision of 2026-10-08 still holds: what depends on his own tests (checks in Altium Designer, KiCad GUI
saves made by him, a run of a real AI agent) is deferred, the code of its change ships, and no evidence
label is raised for it.

**The maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-16.** "Finish all tasks and tests before releasing": c0168 (slotted
holes and cut-out regions in Altium) and c0170 (slots in conversions, placement and routing) are in `0.5.0`,
no longer after it. Two new changes enter `0.5.0`: c0172-altium-draftsman and c0173-board-panels (the KiCad
panel board, the flattened Altium panel, and the Embedded Board Array behind its gate). He chose to hold
`0.5.0` until their native parts pass his Altium kits (round F0, the probes FP and F1 to F8 of c0172's Part
F; Q1 to Q12 of c0173's Part Q), with no `0.5.x` fallback: their rows stay `pending`, and the guard refuses a
verdict while a row is `pending`. Recorded on 2026-10-24 (c0171 task 1.14).

**The maintainer's decision of 2026-10-25.** "Prepare everything to release v0.5; what is open in tests we note
and test for v0.6." `0.5.0` is no longer held for c0172 and c0173: they ship with their native Altium parts
deferred, like every test of his, to v0.6. The release also takes everything done since 2026-10-16: the agent
guide's practice pages (c0174), the viewer `fenolite view` (c0176 to c0191), the compact command page of the guide (c0192) and the viewer's review aids (c0193). The proposal c0175 is not
implemented and is not in `0.5.0`. The rule of 2026-10-08 holds: no evidence label of a deferred task is raised.

## Recorded limits

- **Deferred tasks.** No evidence label of a deferred task was raised: the rows named under "Deferred to
  the next release" and the entries of "Open changes of earlier releases" keep the level their change
  recorded.
- **Conversions.** Every direction is `experimental`; a conversion whose read-back differs from its source
  outside the direction's profile ends with exit 5 and names the difference. The census counts are in
  `docs/evidence/conversion.md`.
- **Slots.** Overlapping slots and cut-outs are merged through the `geo` extra; without it they are refused
  with a named code. In Altium a drilled slot is written as a slot pad; a milled slot or cut-out is reported
  as not lowered while `pcbdoc.CUTOUT_REGIONS` is off (c0168, until S3 is repeated).
- **Panels.** A panel is made of a board whose outline is one rectangle; the Embedded Board Array is not
  written while `pcbdoc.EMBEDDED_ARRAYS` is off (`--altium-panel embedded` exits 7), and the flattened
  panel is written instead (c0173).
- **Draftsman.** `.PCBDwf` documents are read; none is written while `draftsman.NATIVE_WRITE` is off
  (c0172).
- **Viewer.** What `fenolite view` draws of an Altium or KiCad document was reviewed by the coordinator on
  public files only (the browser reviews recorded in each change's tasks); the maintainer's reviews against
