## Requirements
### Requirement: Proposed bounded-safety hypothesis
The change SHALL register `H-G-BOOLEAN-REALIZATION` as pending/proposed with its bounded criterion and external test/receipt names. It MUST retain `INFERRED`, the half-tie limitation and the distinct full-input/production gates; finite passing cases MUST NOT imply universal safety.

#### Scenario: Only bounded proofs pass
- **WHEN** the declared controls and witnesses have a passing receipt
- **THEN** the register records that scope while full176, production review and universal safety remain unproved

### Requirement: Scratch confinement and source identity
The experiment SHALL keep prototype code and raw geometry outside the repository and bind each receipt to exact source, oracle, input and output hashes. It MUST leave production, models, schemas, native files, guards, expected hashes and acceptance unchanged.

#### Scenario: Source-bound external experiment
- **WHEN** the input-audit mode checks the actual176 operands and inherited oracle
- **THEN** its receipt names their verified hashes and the planning commit, and the repository diff contains only authorized planning/evidence documents

### Requirement: Exact signed selection
The reference SHALL node exact Fraction crossings and overlaps, preserve signed subject/clip multiplicities and select faces where subject winding is nonzero and clip winding is zero. It MUST remove only exact collinears before realization and retain rational topology certificates.

#### Scenario: Signed cancellation is retained
- **GIVEN** the actual clip315/316 tiny-face probe has windings+1 and−1
- **WHEN** the scratch reference selects the difference
- **THEN** combined clip winding is zero and no unsigned union replaces that face

### Requirement: One half-even realization
The prototype SHALL call existing `round_point` once per canonical rational vertex, then remove consecutive duplicates and exact collinears and apply existing normal form. It MUST produce deterministic integer polygons or fail an uncertified retained boundary without alternate rounding, coarse grids or tolerance cleanup.

#### Scenario: A retained source turn survives
- **WHEN** the five23-vertex contexts are realized
- **THEN** each certified original source turn remains on its retained integer boundary, or the candidate reports a failed obligation

### Requirement: Every discarded face is justified
The prototype SHALL retain the existing whole-piece/whole-hole debris threshold and order. Every discarded or collapsed face MUST carry exact and integer area/provenance proving eligibility under that policy; unqualified topology loss or removal of a protected large-boundary turn MUST fail the proof.

#### Scenario: A sub-grid selected face collapses
- **GIVEN** the certified tiny face has no integer lattice point in its bounding box
- **WHEN** its single realization collapses
- **THEN** the receipt supplies its whole-face policy certificate instead of silently discarding it or simplifying a neighboring retained boundary

### Requirement: Bounded controls and honest invariances
The experiment SHALL prove the12 controls, faithful23-vertex contexts and75-vertex counterexample with deterministic integers and declared order/direction/start/subdivision/translation checks. It MUST record failed obligations and the half-even half-tie translation counterexample without claiming universal invariance.

#### Scenario: Half ties limit translation symmetry
- **WHEN** the exact values1/2 and3/2 are realized under absolute half-even
- **THEN** outputs0 and2 demonstrate the stated limitation; no origin shift hides it

### Requirement: Escalation and production gates
A full176 experiment SHALL require successful bounded obligations and conservative integer candidate generation checked against exhaustive bounded results. The naive8058-edge Fraction cross product MUST NOT run. Production changes MUST await root review of the concrete proof and a separate implementation design.

#### Scenario: A bounded topology obligation fails
- **WHEN** any retained-face or invariance obligation fails
- **THEN** its task remains open, the full176 run does not start and no production or byte-pin correction is inferred

### Requirement: Contract audit preserves actual precision and failed evidence
A contract audit SHALL distinguish whole-ring orientation and protected nonzero vertices from an experimental local-turn-sign invariant. A new quantization certificate MUST retain half-even projection, squared vertex error at most 1/2 nm², geometric cleanup equality, retained topology and whole-face loss proofs. It MUST NOT rewrite the failed experiment or approve production.

#### Scenario: Quantization changes a local curvature sign
- **GIVEN** an authored exact boundary whose prescribed projection changes a local sign
- **WHEN** the strict experiment and contract audit run
- **THEN** the strict refusal remains recorded and actual geometric obligations are independently checked
- **AND** a missing protected vertex, over-bound displacement or cleanup spike loss still refuses
