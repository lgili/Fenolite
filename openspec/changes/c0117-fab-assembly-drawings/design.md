## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**
- `export` delivers a fabrication drawing and assembly drawings that KiCad draws, from the board as it is after `route` and `fill`, without changing it.
- Every number on a drawing comes from the model read from the board or from KiCad, and the drill counts agree with KiCad's drill files on every run.
- One spec file gives the same drawings on 9.0 and 10.0, and two exports give equal content hashes.

**Non-Goals**
- Everything under "Non-goals" in the proposal.

## Decisions

1. **KiCad draws; Fenolite writes board items into a plot copy.** Tables become `table` items, notes cells of a table, the overall dimensions `dimension` items and missing designators `gr_text` items, all written into a copy of the board that `pcb export pdf --mode-single --include-border-title` plots with its drawing sheet (measurements 3 and 5). Fenolite computes contents and positions, never strokes. Rejected: tables and notes in a `.kicad_wks` per drawing: a sheet holds no table item, so the grid would be Fenolite's lines and texts, and every page would need a sheet file of its own. Rejected: a document that Fenolite assembles from plots: a PDF writer or merger in the stdlib core, and a font that is not KiCad's.

2. **The items exist only in the copy.** The board, its project and its folder never change, as for every kind (living "Export command", "Source"). The copy is built in memory and handed to `KicadCli.run`, whose `files` accept `bytes`. Rejected: writing the items into the board at `build`: the drill table goes stale when `route` adds vias, the stack-up table when Board Setup is edited, and the user would find items that no line of the script wrote (Open Questions).

3. **1:1, the board where it is.** Both majors draw an item at its board coordinates at the default scale, and 9.0.9 offers no other (measurements 1 and 5), so page coordinates are board coordinates and Fenolite places its blocks in them. Rejected: `--scale 0` (10.0.6 only; it centres the box of all board items, footprint texts included, which Fenolite cannot measure, so the free areas are unknown and the title block may cover the content). Rejected: moving the board in the copy: every coordinate of every head, opaque ones included, would move.

4. **The paper.** A page takes the spec's `paper`; with `auto`, the first of A4, A3, A2, A1 and A0 landscape on which its board box (Decision 6) and its blocks fit (Decision 7). The copy's `paper` node is replaced. When no paper fits, or the named one does not, the page gives `drawing.no-room` (error) naming the smallest paper that fits, or that none does, and the board box. Page sizes are those KiCad plots (A4 297.0022 × 210.0072 mm and A3 419.9890 × 297.0022 mm, `H-K-PCB-PAPER` of c0012; A2 to A0 measured by task 1.2). Rejected: the board's own paper by default: the 600-part probe board leaves its A4 page (measurement 8).

5. **The sheet and its obstacles.** The sheet is the spec's `drawing_sheet` (a `.kicad_wks` read by `wks.read_drawing_sheet`, or a `.sheet.toml` built by c0012's templates and written by `wks.write_drawing_sheet` into the run folder; passed with `--drawing-sheet`), else the project's (named by `pcbnew.page_layout_descr_file` and already in the copy set), else KiCad's default. For a known sheet the obstacles are the boxes of the lines and texts that `templates.layout` predicts for the page, and the margin box is the page less the sheet's margins; for KiCad's default the margin box is the page less 10 mm, and the obstacles are the band from there to the inner border at 12 mm and the title block box of measurement 5 (`H-K-DRAW-SHEET`). Content lies in the margin box and meets no obstacle. `exports` may not import `templates`, so `cmd_export` computes the obstacles and passes them as rectangles. A sheet that cannot be read or built gives `drawing.sheet-unread` (error). Rejected: always plotting with the shipped `iso5457_generic`: it lists A4 and A3, its zone letters stop at H, and a user who chose no sheet expects KiCad's.

6. **The fixed content of each page.** The fabrication page plots `Edge.Cuts` and `Dwgs.User`; its board box is the outline's bounding box, grown at the top and left by `dimension_offset` + 2 × `text_size` for the dimensions, joined with the extent of every root item already on `Dwgs.User`, read from the coordinates of its nodes (the user's drawings, c0103's dimensions, KiCad notes). The top assembly page plots `F.Fab` and `Edge.Cuts`; its box joins the outline's with the courtyard boxes of top-side parts and the root items on `F.Fab`. The bottom page plots `B.Fab` and `Edge.Cuts` with `--mirror`; its box, from the bottom side, is mirrored to x ↦ W − x, so it is computed per paper. Every page passes `--drill-shape-opt 0`: the default draws pad holes without via holes (measurement 5), and the drill map shows them all. Rejected: `Cmts.User` or a new user layer for Fenolite's items: whoever draws for fabrication uses `Dwgs.User`, and those items belong on the same page.

7. **Placing blocks.** Blocks are placed in the order board, stack-up, drill, impedance, notes on the fabrication page, and notes on the top assembly page. Each takes the first top-left corner, in the order of x then y, among the margin box's left edge and the right edges of the obstacles, the board box and the placed blocks plus `gap`, and the margin box's top edge and the bottom edges of the same boxes plus `gap`, where it lies in the margin box and stays `gap` away from every obstacle, the board box and every placed block. On the 600-part probe board this puts a 150 × 200 mm block at (263 mm, 17 mm) of an A2 page, A3 being too narrow beside the board. The spec may fix a block's corner (`at`); a fixed block that meets anything gives `drawing.no-room`. The reply lists each block with its corner and size. Rejected: a packing optimiser, whose answer jumps with small edits. Rejected: a fixed column beside the title block, since sheets differ.

8. **Widths are bounds; Fenolite breaks the lines.** Generated texts are `text_size` high (1.5 mm by default) with strokes of 0.15 × the size. KiCad's line pitch is 1.61 × the size, and no glyph of the measured set advances more than 1.45 × the size (measurement 4, `H-K-DRAW-TEXT`); any other character counts 2 × the size. A column is as wide as its widest line by that bound plus two 1 mm margins; a note is broken by Fenolite at spaces, greedily, into lines that fit `notes_width` by the bound (a longer word is cut), and its row is lines × pitch plus the margins. So KiCad never wraps a cell and no row overflows. Rejected: KiCad's wrap, which keeps the row height (measurement 4). Rejected: widths measured from a first plot: exact, but the layout would need a tool run, and its unit tests a fake that sets SVG text lengths.

9. **The tables**, from the model read from the board; lengths in millimetres as exact decimals with at least three decimals (`0.300`, `1.600`, `0.2104`), as KiCad's report prints them:
   - *Board*: copper layer count; outline width × height (bounding box); with a stack-up, its thickness, finish and whether it is impedance-controlled; the smallest drill; with c0112 archived, via protection from `summary(design)`.
   - *Stack-up*: one row per entry of `Board.stackup`, top to bottom, paste entries left out: layer, type (`copper`, `core`, `prepreg`, `dielectric` when unset, `soldermask`, `silkscreen`), material, thickness (empty for 0), Dk (`epsilon_r`), Df (`loss_tangent`), colour; a column empty in every row is dropped; a last row holds `Stackup.thickness()`. Without a stack-up there is no such table, and `drawing.stackup-missing` (info) says so; KiCad's job file then states its default (c0101's `export.stackup-default`).
   - *Drill*: one row per plating, span (first and last copper layer), drill and slot length, with the count and the kinds (`via`, `pad`): pads from `board_pads` (`thru_hole` plated, `np_thru_hole` not; slot length from `Padstack.hole_length`), vias from `Board.vias` (span from `layers`); through spans first, plated before unplated, then other spans in stack order, then by drill and slot length; a total row.
   - *Impedance*: with c0105 archived, the rows of `impedance_table(design)`: target, layer, ohms, tolerance, width, gap, reference layers; no block without a target.
   - *Notes*: the spec's notes, numbered `1.`, `2.`, … in a two-column table without border; `${…}` variables are left to KiCad.

   Rejected: the drill table read from KiCad's report alone: its tool lines differ between majors (measurement 2) and it does not tell a via from a pad. Rejected: a symbol column: the symbols are KiCad's, on the map.

10. **The drill table is checked on every run.** The fabrication kind also runs `pcb export drill … --generate-map --map-format pdf --generate-report` on the copy, keeps the maps and the report, and reads the report: for each drill file, the multiset of (diameter, count) must equal the table rows of that file (plated through, unplated through, each other span). A difference gives `drawing.drill-mismatch` (error), since the drawing would state counts the drill files do not hold; a report that yields no file gives `drawing.drill-report-unread` (warning), and the table stays. Tool lines are read with one or two closing parentheses. How slots and blind or buried pairs appear in the report is probed first (task 1.3), and the comparison follows the probe. Rejected: no check: a wrong count on a fabrication drawing is a defect nobody sees.

11. **Dimensions are `dimension` items.** Two orthogonal dimensions measure the outline's bounding box, horizontal above it and vertical left of it, at `dimension_offset` (8 mm by default), in millimetres with `dimension_precision` decimals (2 by default); KiCad recomputes their text on load (`H-K-DIM`, c0103). The node is c0103's form, built by c0103's emitter when c0103 is archived first, else by this change, and the second to land re-bases on the first. Rejected: lines and texts drawn by Fenolite (c0103's fallback): the number would be Fenolite's, not KiCad's.

12. **Assembly pages.** The top page plots `F.Fab` and `Edge.Cuts`; the bottom page plots `B.Fab` and `Edge.Cuts` with `--mirror`, and is left out, with `drawing.side-empty` (info), when no part sits on the bottom. Both pass `--exclude-value` unless `values = true`, `--sketch-pads-on-fab-layers` when `pads = true`, and for `dnp` `crossout` (default) `--crossout-DNP-footprints-on-fab-layers`, `hide` `--hide-DNP-footprints-on-fab-layers`, `show` nothing (measurement 7). A footprint whose fabrication layer shows no reference (no `${REFERENCE}` text, no visible `Reference` field on that layer) gets a `gr_text` of its reference, `designator_size` high (1 mm by default), at the centre of its courtyard's bounding box (at its position without a courtyard), mirrored on `B.Fab`; under `dnp = "hide"` a do-not-populate part gets none; `drawing.designators-added` (info) counts them. Notes go on the top page, on `F.Fab`. Rejected: plotting the silkscreen for references: it carries more than references, and c0077 puts `Reference` there only for Fenolite's footprints. Rejected: notes on the bottom page: they would need mirrored tables, not measured.

13. **Nodes per target.** Target 10 writes the table's `uuid` and the text box's `(knockout no)`, target 9 neither (token inventory: both since 10.0); the target is the board's major (`versions.inspect`). Uuids are `uuid5` of the page kind and the block and cell names, so one board and one spec give one copy and one PDF. A copy whose layer table lacks a layer the page plots gets its row: `(17 "Dwgs.User" user "User.Drawings")`, `(35 "F.Fab" user)`, `(33 "B.Fab" user)` (`H-K-DRAW-LAYER`, probed by task 1.3). Rejected: building the copy through the model and `write_board`: tables and text boxes are not model entities, and a read board would be rewritten whole for a plot. Rejected: random uuids: two exports of one board would plot different files.

14. **Files, names and hashes.** Under `DIR/drawings/`: `<stem>-fab.pdf`, the map files and `<stem>-drill.rpt` (kind `fab-drawing`), `<stem>-assembly-top.pdf` and `<stem>-assembly-bottom.pdf` (kind `assembly-drawing`). The `.drl` files of the map run are not artefacts (the `drill` kind owns them). Each page passes `-D FENOLITE_DRAWING=<title>` (the spec's titles; by default `Fabrication drawing`, `Assembly drawing, top side`, `Assembly drawing, bottom side`), which any sheet shows with `${FENOLITE_DRAWING}`. `VOLATILE_PREFIXES` gains `/CreationDate` for both kinds and `Created on` for `fab-drawing` (measurements 2 and 6), so two exports of an unchanged board give equal `content_sha256`. c0116 needs the same PDF prefix for its `pdf` kind: the first change to land adds it, the other reuses it. Rejected: one multi-page PDF: the output argument differs per major (measurement 6), and a merge is Fenolite's own PDF code.

15. **The spec file.** `--drawing-spec FILE`: TOML, `schema = "fenolite.drawing-spec.v0"`, read with `tomllib` and `parse_float=Decimal`, closed keys, lengths as strings with units (`core.units.parse_length`), paths relative to the file's folder; every problem in file order in one input error (exit 3, `FEN-3004`) before any run, as c0074 reads presets. Tables: `[page]` (`paper`, `portrait`, `drawing_sheet`, `text_size`, `gap`, `notes_width`), `[fab]` (`title`, `tables`, `dimensions`, `dimension_offset`, `dimension_precision`, `notes`, `at`) and `[assembly]` (`title_top`, `title_bottom`, `sides`, `dnp`, `values`, `pads`, `designators`, `designator_size`, `notes`, `at`). Without a file every default applies and no note is drawn. Rejected: DSL calls: `export` reads the board, not the script, and a KiCad-made board has no script. Rejected: tables in c0074's preset file: a proposal that refuses unknown tables, and drawings are not options of one `kicad-cli` run.

16. **Evidence.** `exports.drawings.EVIDENCE` is `INFERRED` (`H-K-DRAW-ITEMS`, `H-K-DRAW-PAGE`, `H-K-DRAW-DRILL`) and becomes `KICAD-VERIFIED` when the three hold on both majors; the envelope of `export` combines it with `exports.EVIDENCE` when a drawing kind runs. The tables state model values, at the level of the board read; the drill table also passed the report check of the run. Rejected: `exports.EVIDENCE` alone: it covers the file set and the hashes, while a drawing also claims where KiCad draws Fenolite's items.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Tables wider than needed.** The bound of 1.45 × the size is far above the average advance (about 0.8 × the size in the measured notes), so columns carry blank space. It never overflows; a later change may measure per glyph.
- **A board outside the page.** A read board placed off the page gives `drawing.no-room` with its box; the hint says to move it in KiCad. Built boards start at (100 mm, 100 mm).
- **Added references over fabrication graphics.** The text sits at the courtyard's centre at 1 mm; `designator_size` and `designators = false` change it, and footprints that show their reference are untouched.
- **KiCad changes its default sheet or report.** `draw-default-sheet-*` and `draw-drill-counts` fail before a release; a report that cannot be read is a warning, never a wrong count.
- **Run count.** Both drawing kinds add three plots and one drill run, each under 1 s on the 600-part board (measurement 8).

## Open Questions

- **Should `build` write the drawing items into the board on request, so they can be edited in KiCad?** Default: no; the copy is rebuilt on every export.
- **Should `export` find a spec file in the project folder by itself?** Default: no; `--drawing-spec` names it.
- **Should `--all` select the drawing kinds?** Default: no; decided with c0116, which adds the other new kinds.
- **Should notes also go on the bottom assembly page, mirrored?** Default: no, top page only.
- **Should a note naming an undefined variable be reported?** KiCad prints it literally, and 9.0.9's DRC calls it `unresolved_variable`. Default: no check; `docs/drawings.md` says so.
