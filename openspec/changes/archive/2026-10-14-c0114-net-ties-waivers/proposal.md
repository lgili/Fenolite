## Why

The review of 2026-10-05 of the gaps to a complex board (milestone v0.4; `docs/roadmap.md`, "The complex board, c0100–c0120") found three verification gaps that no change owns. All three are open at `origin/dev` `9aba2dff`:

- **Net ties.** A star ground or a Kelvin lead joins pads of two nets on purpose, listed in `net_tie_pad_groups`. Fenolite keeps that child opaque, so its copper check reports tied pads that touch as `copper.short`.
- **No waiver.** A `copper.*` error keeps `check` at exit 5 and refuses `build`; the only escapes are global.
- **Severities and exclusions.** A script cannot set a KiCad DRC severity, and a stale exclusion is dropped silently.

Measured on 9.0.9 and 10.0.6 alike:
- With the token, KiCad judges no pair of pads of the footprint, grouped or not, but reports a mask bridge between ungrouped pads and a track too close to a tied pad.
- An exclusion applies only when its type, its marker position to the nanometre and its ordered uuid pair match. The entry keeps its severity and gains `excluded` and the stored `comment`.

## What Changes

- **Net-tie groups.** `FootprintInstance.net_ties` and `FootprintDef.net_ties`, read from boards and libraries, written for authored footprints (`Footprint.net_tie("1", "2")`). The copper check skips two pads of one group of one footprint.
- **Waivers.** `design.waive(code, *items, reason=…, min_gap=None, name=None)` accepts a `copper.*` or `kicad.drc.*` finding named by its items. The build stores waivers in `.fenolite/findings.json`; `check` and the copper guard keep a matched finding as `info` with the waiver's reason; an unmatched waiver gives `check.waiver-unmatched`.
- **Both targets.** `copper.*` waivers are applied by `check` on a built Altium project and by the copper guard of `build --target altium`, as on KiCad. Net-tie groups and severities are KiCad data: the Altium build writes the pads, writes no severity, and says so once per kind with `altium.not-lowered`; an imported Altium board has no groups.
- **Severities.** `design.rules.severity("kicad.drc.silk-overlap", "ignore")` writes `rule_severities`, for the keys the target major applies.
- **Exclusions.** `check` shows an exclusion's comment and reports `check.exclusion-stale`.

Size: 5.5 design-days.

Limits: shorts are waived by exact names only; a clearance waiver with a glob needs `min_gap`; a DRC waiver has no distance bound; waivers and exclusions of the four types KiCad does not repeat are never reported stale; ungrouped pads of a net-tie footprint stay judged (Fenolite may report more); severities never change `copper.*` findings; `kicad.drc.clearance` cannot be ignored (the canary needs it).

## Non-goals

- Exclusions written from waivers: nowhere, the report lacks the marker position (design, Decision 8).
- Severities or exclusions applied to `copper.*`: nowhere; c0029's reason stands.
- Waivers of `analysis.*`: nowhere. `analyze` judges only against the requirements file the user passes and stores no finding, so the user changes the row; c0115 says the same.
- Waivers of `kicad.erc.*` and `parity.*` findings, and ERC severities: nowhere in this change. An ERC finding has its own means in the script (`no_connect`, a power flag), and a parity finding is a defect of the build. A later change can widen "Waivers in the DSL".
- `check --waivers FILE` for projects Fenolite did not build: nowhere, their own exclusions cover them (an Open Question).
- Net ties written into Altium documents, and net-tie groups read from them: nowhere yet. Both need a format fact from a public source first (`docs/formats/altium/`); until then the Altium build reports the groups and the import reads none, so the copper check of an imported board judges tied pads, which a `copper.short` waiver of a built project accepts by name.
- Altium rule severities: nowhere. A severity is no rule kind, so the Altium rule table of c0084 gains no row; the build reports the severities it does not write.
- A catalog net tie: nowhere, KiCad's `NetTie` library has twelve.
