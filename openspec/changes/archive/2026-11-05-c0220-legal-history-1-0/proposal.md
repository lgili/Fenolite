## Why

The roadmap's v1.0 freeze ends with "`LEGAL.md` reviewed; residue scan green over the whole history"
(`docs/roadmap.md`, "Phase 5: to 1.0" and "Proposed cuts", "Keep for 1.0"). Both have parts today, and
neither is done:

- **The scan.** `tools/residue/scan.py` checks the working tree in CI (`unit`, `wheel`, `release.yml`) and the
  staged files in the pre-commit hook. Its `--history` mode, which reads every blob reachable from any ref,
  runs in no job. Measured on 2026-10-27 on the local `dev` of that day: `--history` scans 10 820 blobs in about 11 minutes
  with 0 hits, both on the public patterns alone and with the private lists of the maintainer's checkout
  (`private gate: on`). Run without those lists, as CI does, it reports "private gate: skipped": the
  private tokens and hashes live outside the repository (`Makefile`: `private/residue-tokens.txt`), so only
  the maintainer's machine runs the whole check.
- **The legal text.** `LEGAL.md` holds blocks A ("Format analysis") and B ("Material from organisations"),
  and `tests/unit/test_legal_docs.py` checks the headings. Nothing records a review. Nothing lists the
  licences of the packages that the extras install. For example, `geo` brings Shapely (BSD-3-Clause) and
  pyclipper (MIT), and the test tools include Hypothesis (MPL-2.0). Nothing records what Fenolite fetches
  and never commits: the KiCad libraries, the pinned Freerouting jar run as a subprocess, and the corpus
  files with the licence of each manifest row.

This is the fifth of the five changes of the freeze (c0216 to c0220), and it is independent of the others.

## What Changes

- **History scan in CI**: `release.yml` and the nightly run `--history` on the public patterns, so that a
  release cannot be published while a reachable blob holds a hit.
- **The private gate before 1.0**: `make residue-history` runs `--history` with the private lists. The
  maintainer runs it on the commit to be released, and records the summary line (counts only) in the release
  record. A hit is handled as `residue-scan` says (a waiver with its reason, or the maintainer's decision to
  rewrite history); no history is rewritten by this change.
- **A dependency licence inventory**: `docs/licences.md`, generated by `tools/gen_licences.py` from `uv.lock`
  and the packages' metadata. It lists each package of each extra and of the runtime with its licence. A test
  refuses a copyleft licence (GPL, AGPL, LGPL) in a package that Fenolite imports in-process.
- **The review record**: `docs/release/v1.0-legal-review.md`. One row per claim of `LEGAL.md`, `NOTICE` and
  the licence of every fetched asset, each with the test or the file that shows it. The maintainer's answer
  and date are recorded; a lawyer's review is not a condition of `1.0.0` (his answer of 2026-10-27).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `residue-scan`: MODIFIED "Integration points".
- `licensing-and-notices`: ADDED "Dependency licence inventory", "Legal review before 1.0".

## Non-goals

- No history rewrite and no change of the patterns or of the private gate's design.
- No legal advice: the record states the project's policy and what shows it.
- The licences of the corpus files are the manifest's (`corpus-policy`); this change only links them.

## Evidence level required

- Mechanical for the workflow steps and the inventory (tests); the review is the maintainer's record, and the
  private-gate run is his, reported as counts.

## Impact

- New: `tools/gen_licences.py`, `docs/licences.md`, `docs/release/v1.0-legal-review.md`, a `Makefile`
  target `residue-history`.
- Changed: `.github/workflows/release.yml`, `.github/workflows/nightly.yml`, `tests/unit/test_ci_workflow.py`,
  `tests/unit/test_legal_docs.py`, `LEGAL.md` (a pointer to the review and the inventory).
- Size: about 3 days, plus the maintainer's review.
