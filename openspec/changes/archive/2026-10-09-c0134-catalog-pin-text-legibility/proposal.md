## Why

On 2026-10-07 the maintainer looked, in Altium Designer, at projects Fenolite writes with the symbols drawn from their own graphics (the default since c0086), and named one defect: on some small components the pin names lie on each other. He named `BJT_NPN`, `Comparator`, `Operational_Amplifier` and `Linear_Regulator` of the catalog and `CONN2` of the example library `FenoliteDemo.kicad_sym`.

The Altium writer honours the symbol's own flags (`altsym.map_pin`), and KiCad draws the same texts. So the cause is in the symbol definitions, and the remedy is there: no writer changes.

Measured before this change on the 64 symbols Fenolite authors: all 24 catalog symbols that show pin names have a text that is hard to read at one of two text sizes; the 25 that hide them have none. Two that hide them, the polarized capacitors, have the number of a pin on the plus mark. In the example library `CONN2` shows each number twice, `DUAL_OPAMP` and `MCU8` hold rotated supply names across their row names, `MCU8` has texts on its inversion bubbles and its clock wedge, and `R_V` has its numbers against its body.

## What Changes

- A rule for every authored symbol, measured by a hermetic test: no shown pin text overlaps another, every shown name has room inside the body, no shown text lies on a stroke of the body or on the mark of a pin shape, no shown name only repeats its pin's number. Each text is a box estimated at KiCad's size (1.27 mm) and at Altium's (the 10-point sheet font).
- Catalog: 20 symbols whose drawing tells the pins apart hide their pin names (the transistors, thyristors and photodetectors, `Schottky_Diode`, `Potentiometer`, `Transformer`, `Switch_SPDT`, `Pushbutton_NO`, `Relay_SPDT`, `Dual_LED_Common_Cathode`, `Optocoupler`, `Bridge_Rectifier`, `Operational_Amplifier`, `Comparator`). The names stay in the model.
- Where a hidden name carried a sign the drawing lacked, strokes carry it: a plus and a minus at the amplifier inputs; a plus, a minus and two waves in the bridge; an arrowhead on the optocoupler's emitter leg. `Schottky_Diode` also gets the pins of `Diode`, and the two polarized capacitors carry their plus mark below the lead, clear of the pin number.
- Catalog: the 4 plain rectangles keep their names and grow to hold them: `Linear_Regulator`, `Offline_Power_Controller`, `Microcontroller`, `Power_Module`.
- Example library: `CONN2` hides its names; `DUAL_OPAMP` and `MCU8` grow; `MCU8` gets longer pins further apart and a name offset past its clock wedge; `R_V` gets longer pins.
- Regenerated by their own tools: seven symbol preview sheets and nine files of the Altium samples `tree`, `kicad_example` and `no_connect`. Boards, nets and designators do not change.
- The generic copies under `tests/data/altium/generic/` keep their bytes; the library they were built from is kept beside them.

Size: 2 design-days.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `fenolite-component-catalog`: ADDED "Legible pin texts"; MODIFIED "Connected, legible schematic symbol drawings" (the strokes that replace hidden names; plain rectangles for the blocks that keep names).

## Non-goals

- No change to a writer, to the model, to a pin number, a pin name, an electrical type or a footprint.
- No change to the mini library `tests/data/libs`: its two symbols that do not pass are listed in the test with their reason.
- No measurement of Altium's text: the Altium size is an assumption, stated in the design.
- No change to where the KiCad schematic writer puts the Reference and Value of a part.

## Evidence level required

- The catalog stays `INFERRED`; no row of `docs/hypotheses.md` changes.
- The rule is measured on Fenolite's model with Fenolite's own estimate. The KiCad size is checked against what `kicad-cli` 10.0.6 draws; the Altium size is not checked in Altium.
- The regenerated Altium files are new bytes that no author report covers; the page that names them says so.

## Impact

- Changed: `src/fenolite/catalog/__init__.py`, `examples/altium_kicad/FenoliteDemo.kicad_sym`, `docs/catalog/`, `docs/evidence/altium-schematic.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, tests of the catalog and of the Altium samples.
- New: `tests/_pintext.py`, `tests/unit/catalog/test_pin_text_legibility.py`, `tests/data/altium/generic/library/FenoliteDemo.kicad_sym`.
- A design that uses a changed symbol gets a different schematic once: the pins of eight symbols move outwards. Its nets and its board stay.
- `src/fenolite/backends/` and `docs/formats/` are not touched.
