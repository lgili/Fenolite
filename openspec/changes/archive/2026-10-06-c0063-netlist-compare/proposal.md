## Why

c0061 writes a schematic, and nothing in Fenolite says what that sheet means electrically. `check` compares the model with the board and the board with KiCad's IPC-D-356 export; the schematic is in neither pair. A generator defect (two pins on one point, a label one grid step off, a hidden power pin that joins nets) would give a project whose sheet says something other than the circuit, and only KiCad's parity warnings would hint at it.

The plan asks for two things in v0.2a: "`netlist` = wrapper" of `kicad-cli sch export netlist` for KiCad projects, and Fenolite's own netlist "only for schematics generated by Fenolite (restricted grammar, 100 % parity required)". Its acceptance: the own netlist equals KiCad's on every generated schematic.

Observed at proposal time on 9.0.9 and 10.0.6: the export is an S-expression with `components` and `nets`; net names and members are equal on both majors; 10.0 adds two root sections and writes `pinfunction` differently; the file carries a date and absolute paths.

## What Changes

- `backends/kicad/netlist.py`: a reader of KiCad's netlist export (components, nets, pins), which ignores the dated and path-bearing parts.
- `backends/kicad/sch_netlist.py`: Fenolite's own netlist of a generated sheet, by coincidence of pin points, labels and flags, and a grammar check that refuses every sheet it cannot judge.
- `build`: a guard. The own netlist of the sheet it is about to write must equal the circuit; otherwise nothing is written.
- `check`: the schematic's netlist, exported by `kicad-cli`, joins `netlist.assignment_compare` as the source `schematic`: against the model on built input, against the board on native input. Model elements are named by pad number (c0056's maps).
- `fenolite netlist PATH` (new, read-only): components and nets of a project as JSON, from `kicad-cli` by default or from Fenolite's own reading with `--source fenolite`.
- Oracle test on both majors: own netlist equal to KiCad's, names included, on the examples and on generated designs.

Size: 6.75 design-days; cut order in the design.

## Non-goals

- No netlist of a schematic Fenolite did not write, except through `kicad-cli` (plan D9: no reimplementation of KiCad's connection graph).
- No netlist file in KiCad's or any other tool's format: the command answers in JSON. Exported files are c0064's and c0065's subject.
- No SPICE, no Altium netlist (v0.3), no hierarchy, no buses.
- No change to parity (c0062) and to the IPC-D-356 pair.

## Evidence level required

- The export reader: `KICAD-VERIFIED (9.0.x, 10.0.x)` for the parts it reads (`H-K-NETLIST-SHAPE`).
- The own netlist: `KICAD-VERIFIED` when it equals the export on every schematic of the test set on both majors (`H-K-NETLIST-OWN`); `INFERRED` until then.
- The `schematic` source of the compare: the oracle's level; the pairs with the model stay `INFERRED` on the model's side, as today.
- The build guard: mechanical; it claims agreement of Fenolite with itself.
- `netlist --source kicad`: `KICAD-VERIFIED`; `--source fenolite`: the own netlist's level.
