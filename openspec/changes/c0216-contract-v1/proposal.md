## Why

The roadmap's v1.0 is a freeze: "CLI, envelope and schemas `v1`" (`docs/roadmap.md`, "Phase 5: to 1.0").
`docs/cli-contract.md` says the contract "is frozen at 1.0; before that, a breaking change bumps the schema
id", and `schemas/README.md` says "after 1.0 only additive changes are allowed". Nothing records what is
frozen: the surface is 49 visible commands with their options, the exit codes 0 to 7, 24 `FEN-` codes and
707 issue codes in 70 tables, each with its severities (measured on 2026-10-29 by `tools/gen_cli_surface.py`,
after the work of c0204 to c0229; 46 commands, 23 codes and 656 issue codes on 2026-10-28). A rename, a dropped option or a raised severity passes
every test today, as long as the command still runs. An agent that drives Fenolite through 1.x needs the
surface written down and every change to it judged.

This change is the first of five for the freeze (c0216 to c0220): the CLI surface, the envelope and the
error object. Result schemas are c0217, the model schema c0218, the conformance matrix c0219, the legal
review and the history scan c0220. The release of `1.0.0` takes a release change of its own, as `0.5.0`
took c0171.

## What Changes

- **The contract surface** `docs/cli-surface.json`, generated by `tools/gen_cli_surface.py` from the
  registered commands and the code registries: every visible command (its `mutates` flag and paged list),
  every option with its type, choices, default and whether it is required, the exit codes, the `FEN-`
  codes and every issue code with its severity, each item marked when it belongs to an experimental feature;
  and the public names of `fenolite.dsl` and `fenolite.api` (their `__all__`), with the parameters of each
  function and the fields of each class, which are frozen with the CLI.
- **A judge of changes** (`fenolite.cli.surface.surface_changes`): against the surface of the last release,
  a removed command, option, choice or code, a changed default or type, a new required option or a raised
  severity is a breaking change and fails `tests/unit/cli/test_cli_surface.py`, unless the item is
  experimental. An addition passes once the file is regenerated.
- **Envelope and error `v1`**: `schemas/fenolite.envelope.v1.json` and `schemas/fenolite.error.v1.json`,
  and the envelope's `schema` becomes `fenolite.<command>.v1`, from one constant
  (`fenolite.cli.api.CONTRACT_VERSION`). The keys do not change. The `v0` files stay, frozen.
- **Deprecation**: an option or a command may be marked deprecated with the release that replaces it; its
  use gives one `cli.deprecated` warning, and `capabilities` lists it. Removal waits for 2.0.
- **A pre-freeze review**: a generated list of the surface (unused options, hidden commands, codes no test
  raises) for the maintainer, who decides what is renamed or dropped before the freeze. Nothing is dropped
  without his answer.
- `docs/cli-contract.md` gains "Stability"; `capabilities` reports `result.contract` (`v1`).

## Non-goals

- No result schema (c0217) and no model schema (c0218).
- No command, option or code is renamed or removed by this change except as the review decides.
- The constant switches to `v1` in the release of `1.0.0`, not before (open question 2).
- No other module than `fenolite.dsl` and `fenolite.api` is frozen: the rest of the package stays internal
  (the maintainer's answer of 2026-10-27).

## Evidence level required

- Mechanical: the surface is generated from the code, and the judge's tests and the consistency suite pass.
  No external tool, no format claim.
