# c0486: a netted copper polygon is copper of its net for the placement optimizer

## Why

`fenolite place --strategy optimize` (c0477) holds a selected part fixed when it cannot prove the part's
copper attachment (`attachment_unchecked`). `cli.placement_protection.inspect_attachments` treats every
board graphic on a copper layer as unsupported copper. It does not look at the graphic's net.

A board converted from Altium holds its solid copper regions as KiCad `gr_poly` items on a copper layer
with a `(net …)`. The KiCad reader keeps that net in the graphic's slots and leaves `Graphic.net_id` at
`None` (c0277, c0412). So one such polygon anywhere on the board makes every selected part
`attachment_unchecked`, and the optimizer moves nothing. On the maintainer's board (private; codes and
counts only) every selected part was held fixed this way.

A minor doc issue goes with it. The help of `place --move` and the guide say "Y down" and nothing more.
Users who come from Altium (Y up) or read file coordinates pass the wrong sign. The direction of a larger
Y, and of a negative Y, is not stated.

## What Changes

- The optimizer projects the nets of copper graphics (`model.graphicnets.with_graphic_nets`, which a
  KiCad design registers as `fpitems.with_graphic_nets`) before the attachment inspection.
- `inspect_attachments` reads a copper graphic with a net as copper of that net: a pad it touches is
  `attached_copper`. Only a copper graphic with no net, or of a kind it cannot shape, keeps the part
  `attachment_unchecked`.
- The help of `--move` (and of `--region`, which shares the frame) and the guide pages say which way Y
  grows: a larger Y moves the part down, toward the bottom edge; a negative Y lies above the outline's
  top-left corner. A test checks the documented direction on a real move.

## Impact

- `src/fenolite/cli/place_optimize.py` (`propose`), `src/fenolite/cli/placement_protection.py`.
- `src/fenolite/cli/cmd_place.py` (help text), `src/fenolite/agent/skill/references/placement.md`,
  `commands-4.md` (regenerated by `tools/gen_agent_guide.py`).
- No change for a board without copper graphics. Tidy (`tidy.graph.net_pieces`) is not changed.
