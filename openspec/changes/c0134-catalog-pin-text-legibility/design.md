## Context

- **Report.** On 2026-10-07 the maintainer looked at Altium projects written with the symbols' own graphics (c0086) and named one defect: on some small components the pin names lie on each other. He named `BJT_NPN`, `Comparator`, `Operational_Amplifier`, `Linear_Regulator` and `CONN2`.
- **Where the cause is.** `backends/altium/altsym.map_pin` shows a name unless the symbol hides its names or the name is `""` or `~`, and a number unless the symbol hides its numbers. `backends/kicad/sym.write_symbol_library` writes the same two flags. Both writers do what the definition says, so the definitions are what changes.
- **What exists.** 49 catalog symbols (`src/fenolite/catalog/`, c0075 and c0076), the example library `examples/altium_kicad/FenoliteDemo.kicad_sym` (4 symbols, c0034) and the mini library `tests/data/libs/` (5 and 6 symbols in its two files), which the other examples place. The requirement "Connected, legible schematic symbol drawings" asks for readable labels but measures nothing.
- **Measured before this change** (the rule of Decision 1 on the 64 definitions): 30 have no finding and 34 have one. All 24 catalog symbols that show names are among the 34, and so are two of the 25 that hide them, `Capacitor_Polarized` and `Capacitor_Electrolytic`, whose number `1` lies on the plus mark. The other eight are the four symbols of the example library, and `Mini_DualGate` and `Mini_QFP32_IC` in both files of the mini library.
- **Constraints.** No writer changes. Integer nanometres. The symbols are authored here: no library of another origin was opened or copied. Pin numbers, pin names, electrical types and footprints keep their values.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A rule that says when a shown pin text is legible, and a hermetic test that measures it on every authored symbol.
- The five named symbols, and every other symbol with the same defect, changed by that rule.
- Every file that depends on a changed definition regenerated by its own tool, and each changed byte accounted for.

**Non-Goals:**
- Everything under "Non-goals" in the proposal.
- A claim about what Altium draws. The Altium size is an assumption.

## Decisions

1. **The rule.** Per unit, in body style 1, a symbol shows the name of each pin that is not hidden (unless the symbol hides its names or the name is empty) and the number of each such pin (unless it hides its numbers). Each shown text has a box. Six findings:
   - `REPEAT`: a shown name equals the shown number of its own pin;
   - `UNCHECKED`: a shown text holds a character whose box was not compared with a drawing (Decision 2);
   - `OVERLAP`: two boxes share more than 0.05 mm in both directions;
   - `OUTSIDE`: the box of a name drawn inside the body leaves the box of the body's graphics;
   - `STROKE`: a stroke of the body, or the stem of another pin, passes through the box of a name or of a number;
   - `MARK`: the mark of a pin's shape (an inversion bubble, a clock wedge, a low-level mark) passes through the box of a name or of a number, of that pin or of another.

   A symbol passes with no finding at both sizes of Decision 2. `OUTSIDE` and `STROKE` together are "room inside the body": a transistor's `B` lies inside the box of its strokes and on the base lead. The first form of the rule judged names only against strokes and knew no pin shape; the review of 2026-10-07 showed both gaps in KiCad's drawing (`MCU8`: the clock wedge through the `C` of `CLK`, the numbers on the bubbles), and the rule took them in.

2. **The estimate** (`tests/_pintext.py`, integer nanometres, a segment clipped exactly with fractions).

   | | KiCad size | Altium size |
   |---|---|---|
   | text asked for | 1.27 mm, the size `kicad/sym.py` writes | the 10-point sheet font of `altium/schlib.py` |
   | thickness of a line | 1.6 mm | 90 mil |
   | width of a character | 1.15 × 1.27 mm = 1.4605 mm | 100 mil |
   | a name | from the symbol's name offset (0.508 mm when it has none) past the body end, along the pin | from 50 mil inside the body end, along the pin |
   | a number | centred on the middle of the pin, from 0.36 mm clear of the pin line | from 80 mil outside the body end, 10 mil clear of the pin line |
   | an overbar | 0.5 mm more on the side the letter tops point to | nothing |
   | the mark of a pin shape | the strokes KiCad draws | the same strokes, assumed |

   - **KiCad size: compared with KiCad.** Every catalog symbol and the four symbols of the example library were placed alone, built, and drawn by `kicad-cli` 10.0.6 (`sch export svg`); the SVG holds each text as glyph strokes. Result for the 205 pin texts drawn: every stroke lies inside its box by at least 0.09 mm, more than half the pen of 0.15 mm. A name of two or more characters is at most 0.92 text heights per character long, one digit at most 1.05 high. The strokes of a name reach 0.69 mm to the side its tops point to and 0.58 mm to the other; an overbar lies 1.04 mm from the axis; the strokes of a number lie from 0.52 mm to 1.85 mm clear of the pin line. The thickness of 1.6 mm, the 0.36 mm and the 0.5 mm come from these numbers. The comparison is a scratch run of this change, not a repository test.
   - **The KiCad width holds for the characters that were compared, and for no other.** The 205 texts are written with capitals, digits, `+`, `-`, `_` and `~` (`CHECKED` in the module). A reviewer drew two names of four `@` and four `%` whose boxes just meet, and KiCad drew them into each other; and a name with descenders (`gypqj`) 1.6 mm above a row of `T`, which touched it with no finding. So a shown text with another character is the finding `UNCHECKED`: the estimate does not pass what it was not compared on. No authored symbol has one.
   - **Pin shapes: measured in KiCad.** Each of the nine pin shapes was drawn by `kicad-cli` 10.0.6 at the four pin angles (`sym export svg` of a scratch library, 2026-10-07). An inversion bubble is a circle of 1.27 mm outside the body end; a clock wedge is 1.27 mm wide at the body's edge and reaches 1.27 mm into the body; the low-level marks are 1.27 mm long and 1.27 mm high and lie above a level pin and left of an upright one, on the side of the pin's number; the outer wedge reaches 1.27 mm out; the cross of a non-logic pin spans 1.27 mm around the body end. `pin_marks` holds these strokes and a test pins them. The wedge is deeper than KiCad's default name offset of 0.508 mm, so a clock pin needs a name offset beyond 1.27 mm; a bubble takes half of a pin of 2.54 mm, where KiCad centres the number. What Altium draws for the edge codes the Altium writer sets is not known: the same strokes are assumed.
   - **Altium size: assumed.** Nothing was measured in Altium. The Altium layout counts 70 mil per character (`symbols.CHAR_WIDTH`, "a Fenolite estimate") and a line of 100 mil (`layout.TEXT_HEIGHT`). In a proportional face a digit is about half the nominal size wide and a capital about two thirds; pin names are capitals. If Altium draws 10 points at their nominal size (139 mil), a capital takes 90 to 100 mil and 70 mil fits digits only; if it draws them smaller, 70 mil is about right. The rule takes 100 mil, which holds for both readings except for names with an `M` or a `W`. The two margins (50 and 80 mil) and names running along vertical pins are Altium's defaults as far as known; they are not recorded as format facts.
   - **Supporting observation, not Altium.** `kicad-cli` 10.0.6, converting the regenerated `tree.SchLib` (`sym upgrade`), gives the two 10-point texts of each symbol, its designator and its comment, the size 1.8288 mm (72 mil). If a capital of Altium's 10-point text is about that high, it is about as wide, and 100 mil per character is on the safe side by a quarter. This says what KiCad's importer assumes, not what Altium draws.
   - **Differences from the measurement taken before the change** (it is the origin of this estimate): the Altium width is 100 mil instead of 70; the KiCad line is 1.6 mm thick instead of 1.27 and an overbar counts, both from the comparison above, which found numbers up to 0.22 mm and overbars 0.41 mm outside the thinner box; the stem of another pin counts as a stroke; a number is judged against strokes as a name is (the first measurement had this as `NUMSTROKE`); a symbol with names outside (`offset 0`) is not judged against the body's box; and the measure works on the model, so the two catalog symbols with a pin off the 10-mil grid are measured at the Altium size too.

3. **Drawings hide, plain boxes show.** In the catalog a body that is one filled rectangle says nothing about its pins, so it shows their names; any other body is a drawing that tells them apart, so it hides them. `catalog._NAMED_BLOCKS` names the four rectangles; `pin_names_hidden` is `spec.name not in _NAMED_BLOCKS`. A test checks the equivalence on all 49 symbols without naming any. Hiding changes the flag only: every name stays in the model, so `part["GND"]` builds as before.

   | symbol | before | what tells the pins apart | drawing |

   | `IGBT` | as the BJT | gate plate, emitter arrow | unchanged |
   | `MOSFET_N_Channel`, `MOSFET_P_Channel` | K, A: `G`, `D`, `S` on strokes; A: `D` on `S` | gate plate, body arrow, body diode | unchanged |
   | `SCR`, `TRIAC` | K, A: names on each other and on strokes | triangle and bar; the gate lead enters at the cathode or `MT1` side | unchanged |
   | `Schottky_Diode` | K: `A` on `K`; A: outside | triangle and hooked bar | unchanged; pins as below |
   | `Photodiode`, `Phototransistor` | names on strokes or outside | triangle and bar; emitter arrow | unchanged |
   | `Potentiometer` | names on the zigzag and the wiper | the wiper arrow; the two ends are alike | unchanged |
   | `Transformer` | `P1` on `P2`, `S1` on `S2` | two windings with phase dots | unchanged |
   | `Switch_SPDT`, `Pushbutton_NO`, `Relay_SPDT` | names on each other and on the contacts | lever, bars, coil and resting contact | unchanged |
   | `Dual_LED_Common_Cathode` | names struck through by the leads | two diodes, one joined cathode | unchanged |
   | `Optocoupler` | names on the internal leads | the LED; for the detector nothing told `C` from `E` | plus 2 strokes |
   | `Bridge_Rectifier` | K: the four names cross the diamond at its corners | diode directions | plus 9 strokes |
   | `Operational_Amplifier`, `Comparator` | K: `V+` on `V-`; A: all four names on each other and on the triangle | nothing told the inputs apart | plus 3 strokes |

   - **Strokes that replace a hidden name**, all 0.254 mm wide, lines only:
     - amplifiers: a plus at the non-inverting input, (−2.94, 2.54)–(−2.14, 2.54) and (−2.54, 2.14)–(−2.54, 2.94) mm, and a minus at the inverting input, (−2.94, −2.54)–(−2.14, −2.54). The supply stems carry no mark: the positive one is the upper one, as before.
     - bridge: a plus centred (0, 3.5) with arms of 0.4 mm, a minus centred (0, −3.5), and a wave of three strokes from x = ∓3.95 to ∓3.05 mm between y = ±0.15 mm beside each AC corner. KiCad drew the old `-` of the bottom pin turned on its side, and the Altium writer never shows a `~`; the strokes are upright and equal in both tools.
     - optocoupler: an open arrowhead at the outer end of the lower leg, (3.2, −2.54)–(3.037, −1.737) and (3.2, −2.54)–(2.498, −2.118) mm, 24° to each side of the leg. Lines, because the symbol holds exactly one polygon, its LED.
   - **`Schottky_Diode` also takes the pins of the catalog's `Diode`** (ends at ±5.08 mm, 3.81 mm long; before ±3.81 mm, 2.54 mm long). With the short pin KiCad draws the number `2` on the cathode hook, which reaches 0.635 mm past the bar (seen in the drawing). Since the review the rule measures it: the symbol with its old pins and hidden names has the finding `STROKE number '2'`.
   - **`Capacitor_Polarized` and `Capacitor_Electrolytic` carry their plus mark below the positive lead** (centre (−2.2, −1.9) mm; before (−2.2, 1.9) mm). KiCad draws the number `1` above that lead, so the mark stood against it and read `1+`. Nothing else of the two symbols changes; they hid their names before.

5. **Catalog symbols that keep their names** (4). Each body is a rectangle around the origin, with its pins on the 2.54 mm grid, that passes the rule at both sizes; two of the four are the smallest that does. Sizes in units of 1.27 mm (50 mil).

   | symbol | before | after | tried and failing at the Altium size | room left at the Altium size |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | `Linear_Regulator` | 6 × 6, pins 3.81 mm | 12 × 12: `IN` and `OUT` on the row 5.08 mm above the centre, `GND` up from the bottom edge | 8 × 8, 12 × 8; 12 × 12 with the row at the centre | `IN` and `OUT` meet; `GND` ends 105 mil below their row (5 mil with the row 2.54 mm above the centre) |
   | `Offline_Power_Controller` | 8 × 8 | 16 × 8 | 8 × 8 | 200 mil between `VIN` and `SW` and between `GND` and `FB` |
   | `Microcontroller` | 12 × 12 | 20 × 12 | 12 × 12, 16 × 12 (`RESET` on `ADC`) | 100 mil between `RESET` and `ADC` |
   | `Power_Module` | 12 × 12 | 16 × 12 | 12 × 12 (`DC-` on `IN1`) | 100 mil between `DC-` and `IN1` |

   - Two names that "meet" touch at the 100 mil width; a face that draws `I` narrower than `N` leaves a gap there. A wider `Linear_Regulator` (16 × 12) was not taken, to keep the block of three pins a small square.
   - `Offline_Power_Controller` passes at 12 × 8, where its two rows of facing names meet. It was 12 × 8 until the review, whose own estimate of a 10-point proportional face left a third of a character between `GND` and `FB`; 16 × 8 leaves 200 mil at the rule's width.
   - `Linear_Regulator` is the other body that is not the smallest that passes: 12 × 10 with the row of `IN` and `OUT` 2.54 mm below the top edge passes too, with 5 mil between `GND` and that row and a body that is not centred on the origin. The square leaves 105 mil and is centred like every other block.
   - All four are clean at the KiCad size with more than 0.9 mm between any two texts. They had no finding at the KiCad size before, except `Linear_Regulator`.

6. **Example library** (`FenoliteDemo.kicad_sym`, edited by hand as it was authored).
   - `CONN2`: `(pin_names (hide yes))`, as `R_V` of the same file has. Its names `1` and `2` repeated its numbers.
   - `DUAL_OPAMP`: both unit rectangles 20 × 16 units, (−12.7, 10.16) to (12.7, −10.16) mm; inputs at x = −15.24, output at x = 15.24, `VCC` at (0, 12.7), `VEE` at (0, −12.7). 16 × 12, 16 × 16 and 20 × 12 fail at the Altium size (the rotated supply names against the row names).
   - `MCU8`: 20 × 16 units, (−12.7, 10.16) to (12.7, −10.16) mm, the size of `DUAL_OPAMP`. Every pin is 5.08 mm long: left pins at x = −17.78 and y = 5.08, 0 and −5.08; right pins at x = 17.78, `OE` at y = 5.08 and `OUT` at 0; `VDD` at (0, 15.24), `GND` at (0, −15.24), the hidden `TP` at (2.54, −15.24). It states a name offset of 1.778 mm, `(pin_names (offset 1.778))`. The first form of this change made it 16 × 16 with pins of 2.54 mm on a pitch of 2.54 mm; in KiCad's drawing the clock wedge of pin 3 then went through the `C` of `CLK`, the numbers 2 and 5 touched their bubbles, the number 3 touched the bubble of pin 2, and the overbar of `RST` lay under `VCC` like an underline (review of 2026-10-07). The offset puts the names past the wedge, the longer pins put each number beyond its bubble, and the pitch separates the rows. At the Altium size the rows of `VCC` and `CLK` end 105 mil from the columns of `VDD` and `GND`.
   - `R_V`: its two pins are 2.54 mm long, with their ends at (0, ±5.08) mm (before 1.27 mm, at ±3.81). KiCad drew the numbers `1` and `2` against the corners of the body. The body and the hidden names are as they were.
   - `DUAL_OPAMP` and `MCU8` keep their pin rows, the pins on four sides, the inverted and clock shapes, the overbars, the hidden pin and the common supply pins. Their `Reference` and `Value` fields move from y = ±6.35 to ±11.43 mm, 1.27 mm beyond the new bodies as beyond the old ones.

A new test builds today's library in the generic form and reads it back to the example's nets.
   - Rejected: regenerating the five generic copies. It would end the statement that they are the bytes of that commit and need the pin in `test_altium_pcb_complete.py` loosened.

8. **Not done, and why.**
   - **Mini library.** `Mini_DualGate` (its power unit has no body: `GND` lies on `VCC` at any size) and `Mini_QFP32_IC` (three rows overlap at the Altium size; clean at the KiCad size) stay. The library is the fixture of the reader's tests and feeds the committed `blink`, `routed`, `board6` and hierarchy samples, which author reports partly cover. Both are listed in `KNOWN` of the test with these reasons.
   - **Numbers beside a mark.** The number `2` of `Thermistor_NTC` and `Thermistor_PTC` stands right under the `T` mark of the body and can be read as its subscript. No stroke passes through its box, so it is not a finding, and the two symbols stay.
   - **The writers' own pin name offset.** `write_symbol_library` writes the name offset 0.508 mm for every symbol; the schematic writer embeds a symbol's own offset, so the drawing of `MCU8` on a sheet is right. A library written from a definition with another offset would lose it.
   - **Fields.** The KiCad schematic writer puts the Value of a low part on the number of its first pin (`CONN2`, `Resistor`) and lets a long Value run into the next part in the grid layout. That is `schlayout`, not a definition.
   - **The Altium writer's own estimate** (`symbols.CHAR_WIDTH` = 70 mil) and `altsym.map_pin`, which shows a name equal to its designator while the generic body does not.
   - **`Potentiometer` and `Terminal_1Pin`** keep a pin off the 10-mil grid; the Altium build still reports `altium.symbol-off-grid` for them.

9. **Nets and boards.** No pin number, name, type or unit changes, so no net can change. Shown three ways, before (commit a343cf87, the tip of `dev` when the work began; the 13 commits `dev` gained since, up to 17234727, change no file of the catalog, of the examples, of the schematic writers or under `tests/data`) and after:

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Altium draws its text wider than 100 mil per character, or with other margins] → names could still touch in the two blocks where they meet at the estimate. One look settles it: `U2` of the tree sample (`IN`, `OUT`, `GND`).
- [Altium draws its text narrower] → the six bodies are larger than needed; they can shrink once the width is known.
- [A user's schematic changes] → once, on the next build: the pins of eight symbols move outwards (the four blocks, `Schottky_Diode`, `DUAL_OPAMP`, `MCU8`, `R_V`). Nets, designators and boards stay.
- [A unit is judged against strokes it does not draw, and an alternate body style is not judged] → the model does not say which unit draws a graphic, so the strokes of all units are pooled, which can only add findings; body style 2 of a symbol passes unmeasured. Of the authored symbols only `Mini_DualGate` of the mini library has a second body style, and it is listed in `KNOWN` for its power unit; the two-unit `DUAL_OPAMP` draws the same rectangle in both units.
- [Altium draws the marks of a clock or an inverted pin larger than KiCad does, or keeps the name at its margin beside the wedge] → the name `CLK` of `MCU8` could touch its wedge in Altium; the writers were not changed. One look at `U1` of the `no_connect` sample settles it.
- [A hidden name is missed] → the role line of the preview sheets and `fenolite catalog show` still list every name; the amplifier, bridge and optocoupler carry strokes for what the drawing did not say.

## Migration Plan

None for users: rebuild. To roll back, revert the commit; the generic copies and the kept library do not depend on it.
