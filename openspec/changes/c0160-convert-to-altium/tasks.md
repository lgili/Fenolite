Two new constants, `DIFAT_ENTRIES_PER_SECTOR` (127 = 128 − 1) and `DIFSECT`, and two helpers: `_difat_sectors(fat_sectors)` and `_difat_bytes(fat_sectors, difat_sectors)`. The FAT-sector count and the DIFAT-sector count settle together in one monotone loop (each step adds 128 entries of capacity and at most one sector of need), so the layout is still header, FAT, DIFAT, directory, mini FAT, mini stream, large streams, every chain consecutive. Each DIFAT sector holds the FAT sector numbers past the header's 109, 127 of them and the rest `FREESECT`, then the number of the next DIFAT sector (`ENDOFCHAIN` in the last), the FAT marks each `DIFSECT`, and the header names the first DIFAT sector and their count (the sector right after the FAT sectors). **Byte-neutral where no DIFAT sector is needed**: with `fat_sectors ≤ 109` the DIFAT count is 0, `first_difat` is `ENDOFCHAIN`, no FAT entry is `DIFSECT`, the chain cursor starts where it did and no part is added, so every committed sample keeps its bytes (the golden hashes of `test_lower_build_spec.py` and of the whole `tests/unit/backends/altium` suite pass unchanged).
