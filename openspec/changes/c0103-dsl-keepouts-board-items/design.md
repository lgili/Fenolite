## Context

**Scope.** The review of 2026-10-05 of the gaps to a complex board found no owner for three gaps of a board defined by its script: keep-outs and rule areas, board text, drawings and dimensions, and rules scoped to an area (its examples: BGA neck-down, a high-voltage section). This change, c0103 of milestone v0.4 (`docs/roadmap.md`, "v0.4: proposals on other branches"), takes them, on top of the rule constructor and selectors of c0071 (archived).

**What exists** (`origin/dev` at `9aba2dff`, read on 2026-10-07):

- *Model* (`model/board.py:298-330`, `model/rules.py:36-48`). `Keepout(outline, layers, no_tracks, no_vias, no_pads, no_copper_pour, no_footprints)` has no name. `Text(text, position, layer, size, thickness, rotation)` has no justification. `Graphic(kind, layer, points, width, filled)` covers `line`, `arc`, `circle`, `rect` and `polygon`. No entity holds a dimension. `RuleSubject` is `(item_kind, net, netclass, ref, layer)`; `SelectorOp` is `all`, `net`, `netclass`, `ref`, `layer`, `item_kind`, `and`, `or` and `not`. c0126 (open on `dev`) puts `Text` and `Graphic` entities inside footprint instances as well.
- *Reader* (`backends/kicad/pcb.py`). A `zone` with a `keepout` child becomes a `Keepout`; its `name` child stays opaque, because `KEEPOUT_FIELDS` leaves `name` out. `gr_text` becomes a `Text` whose size and thickness are projected from `effects`. `dimension` stays an opaque root child, and its own `gr_text` carries its uuid (`H-K-PCB-UUID` counts 13 such repeats in 6 demo boards).
- *Writer.* It creates rule areas (a `zone` with `keepout` and `polygon`, no `name`), texts (`effects` with the font, `(justify mirror)` on a back layer) and drawings (`stroke`, `fill`), in `pcb.CANONICAL_ORDER`.
- *DSL.* No call creates any of them: `to_model` gives `Board(outline, zones)` only (`dsl/convert.py:186`), and `fenolite.dsl.KEYS` (`:35`) has no row for an area, a text, a graphic or a dimension. `design.rules.rule()` and `fenolite.dsl.select` (c0071) offer the leaves `net`, `netclass`, `ref` and `item`; `rule()` refuses an undeclared net class at the call. c0096, implemented on its own branch, adds `Design.keepout(key, …)` ("Relation to c0096").
- *Rules* (`backends/kicad/rulemap.py:78-116`). `KIND_SELECTORS` narrows the grammar per kind and `SELECTOR_SUPPORT` holds `{9, 10}` for its ten keys; both are living text since c0071 was archived. `parse_condition` lifts `memberOfFootprint` and the properties `NetName`, `NetClass`, `Type` and, for courtyard rules, `Reference`; any other function keeps the rule opaque with `rules.kept-opaque`. `copperrules.opaque_clearance_rules` counts such clearance rules, and the copper stage reports them as `copper.rules-incomplete`.
- *Copper check* (`checks/copper.py`, `checks/clearance.py:109-183`). Leaf values and subject names are compared without letter case. `resolve(a, b, *, zone_clearance=None)` also weighs the clearance of a fill's zone (c0068, archived). Keep-outs are not read.
- *Lens* (`lens/preserve.py`). `merge_layout` keeps the existing board's rule areas, texts and drawings and drops those of the built design ("Copper items follow their nets", "Board content outside the design is kept"). Script copper is regenerated by its marked uuid (`copper_uuid`, marker `fenoli`, "Script copper in a merge"); script zones are matched by uuid and the board wins unless the zone is locked ("Zones declared in the script").
- *Altium build* (c0084, c0085 and c0088, open on `dev` with their code merged). A build that plans no PCB document reports keep-outs, texts, graphics and holes with one `altium.not-lowered` per kind (`lens/altium_copper.py:476-498`, `BOARD_KINDS`). A build that plans one writes them through `lower_items` (`:516`), reports each item that has no record with `where` `<kind>/<id>`, and counts both by the closed tuple `KINDS` (`:82`: `text`, `graphic`, `keep-out`, `hole` among them) in `result.pcb`. A keep-out with no restriction that the record carries is not written (`pcbdoc.keepout_problem`), and `no_footprints` is reported as lost. The text record holds no justification. Rules are lowered through `backends/altium/rulemap.py`: a selector leaf other than `net` and `netclass` gives the reason `scope-unsupported` for that rule (`:279`). The copper guard of an Altium build (c0088) runs `check_copper` on the document read back; only a short stops the build.

**Measured on 2026-10-05,** on the branch where this proposal was written (`review-roadmap-complex-board`); not repeated on `dev` at `9aba2dff`, and tasks 1.2 and 8.1 record every part again as a probe. 35 benches built with the model API and `write_board` for each target, on the rules bench of `tests/kicad/rules/_rulebench.py` with c0071's scoped canary; features the writer lacks (an area name, a justification, a dimension) were patched into the written text. **Constraints.** Stdlib only, integer nanometres and microdegrees, no float, no shipped value; the DSL imports only `core` and `model`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals**
- A script declares named rule areas and keep-outs, board texts, drawings and dimensions, and scopes rules to an area; the build writes them for KiCad 9 and 10.
- Fenolite's copper check applies area rules and keep-outs as KiCad's DRC does, so `build`'s copper guard stops what DRC would report.
- A rebuild takes the script's items from the script and keeps what was drawn in KiCad.

**Non-Goals**
- Everything under "Non-goals" in the proposal, with the destinations named there and in Decisions 1, 3 and 5.

## Decisions

1. **One call for rule areas, with `forbid`.** `design.rule_area(name, outline, *, layers=None, forbid=()) -> RuleArea`. KiCad stores a named area for rules and a keep-out as the same `zone` with a `keepout` child (measurements 1–3); an area that forbids nothing serves rules only. `forbid` takes `tracks`, `vias`, `pads` and `pours`, mapped to `no_tracks`, `no_vias`, `no_pads` and `no_copper_pour`. `layers` defaults to every copper layer of the board. A name matches `^[A-Za-z0-9_.+-]+$`, which no condition string needs to escape and which holds no `*`. Names are unique, and two names that differ only in letter case are refused: KiCad compares area names with case (measurement 3), so a rule written for one would silently miss the other.
   - `footprints` is not a value of `forbid`: c0113 owns areas that forbid placement. It may add the value to the same tuple; the writer already writes `Keepout.no_footprints`.
   - c0102 sends keep-outs around mounting holes here: such a keep-out is a `rule_area` whose outline is a polygon around the hole. Model outlines hold points only, so a round keep-out is a polygon; a helper that makes one from a centre and a radius can follow when a script needs it.
   - Rejected: a `keepout()` call and an `area()` call, two names for one KiCad object. c0096 implemented a `keepout()` call of its own on its branch; the maintainer decided on 2026-10-07 that `rule_area` stands ("Relation to c0096"). Rejected: five keyword flags (`no_tracks=True`, …), which c0113 would have to extend with a keyword of its own.

2. **`Keepout.name`, not a new entity.** The name is a child of the rule area (measurement 2). The model adds `name: str = ""`, the reader models the child and the writer writes it after `uuid` when it is not empty, where 10.0.6 keeps it. The re-save adds five children to a created area (measurement 2); the writer does not write them, since a created area without them loads on both majors. Rejected: a `RuleArea` model entity beside `Keepout`, which would split every reader of rule areas (c0074's stitching, c0107's router input).

3. **The area selector: intersection, case-sensitive, with globs.** `Selector("area", v)` is written `S.intersectsArea('v')` and lifted back from hand-written rules. `SELECTOR_SUPPORT["area"]` holds the majors of the probe `dru-cond-area` (`{9, 10}`, measurement 3). `area` joins the leaves of the six kinds of v0.1 and of `hole_to_hole`, `hole_clearance` and `annular_width` in `KIND_SELECTORS`. It was measured on clearance (one side and both), track width and hole to hole; the other kinds follow c0018's rule that a key proven once is written for every kind whose grammar takes it.
   - Rejected: lowering `area` to `enclosedByArea`, which drops the items that cross the area's edge (measurement 3), the very tracks a neck-down rule is written for.
   - Not offered, nowhere: `enclosedByArea` as a second selector, because the review's two cases (neck-down, high-voltage clearance) need intersection; it loads and selects on both majors (measurement 3), so a selector can join when a case needs it. `insideArea` loads too. Rules with either stay opaque on read.
   - Not offered, nowhere: an area on `creepage`, because the high-voltage nets form a net class, which the creepage grammar of c0071 already takes. Not on `courtyard_clearance` and `silk_clearance` either, whose grammars select footprints or the whole board (c0071).
   - Not offered, nowhere: component-class selectors (the review's other half of the gap). A component class comes from a schematic field or from `assign_component_class`, a rules keyword of 10.0 only (`docs/formats/kicad/rules.md`); `ref` globs and areas select the parts of the review's cases.

4. **An unknown area stops the build, after the merge.** KiCad loads a condition that names no area and selects nothing (measurement 3), which would pass a board the user believes is checked. `build_design` checks the layout it is about to write, script areas and areas kept from the board alike: every `area` leaf of every rule must match the `name` of at least one `Keepout` with `fnmatchcase`, else `build.area-unknown` (error) names the rule and the value. `rule()` does not check area names at the call, unlike net classes: an area drawn in KiCad is known only after the merge. Rejected: a check in the lowering, which sees no board. Rejected: a check at the call, which would refuse areas drawn in KiCad.

5. **Drawings: seven calls, keys, layers by kind.** `text`, `line`, `rect`, `circle`, `arc`, `polygon` and `dimension`, with the argument names of the footprint DSL (`line(start, end)`, `rect(start, end)`, `circle(center, edge)`, `polygon(points)`) and a key first. Keys follow the pattern of copper intents and are unique across the seven calls, so a key names one drawing and its uuid survives the insertion of another call.
   - Layers: those of kind `silkscreen`, `soldermask`, `fabrication` and `user` (`F.SilkS`, `B.SilkS`, `F.Mask`, `B.Mask`, `F.Fab`, `B.Fab`, `Dwgs.User`, `Cmts.User`, `Eco1.User`, `Eco2.User` on a created board), chosen by kind so that layers a later change adds follow.
   - Refused, nowhere: copper. A copper text shorts a track and a copper line across one is not reported (measurements 4 and 5), and the copper check sees neither. Refused: `Edge.Cuts`, owned by c0102's outline. Refused, nowhere: courtyard, paste, adhesive and `Margin`, on which no case of the review draws.
   - Defaults: a text 1 mm high with a 0.15 mm stroke, the values the writer gives created footprint fields (`pcb.TEXT_SIZE`, `pcb.TEXT_THICKNESS`), repeated in `dsl/items.py` because the DSL imports no backend; a dimension line of 0.1 mm (a Fenolite choice, in the writer).
   - A text is printable and holds no control character, so one call writes one line; c0117 decides whether its notes need several lines in one text.
   - Rejected: ids by position, which would change every later uuid when a call is inserted. Rejected: copper with a warning.

6. **Justification of board text.** `Text.h_justify` and `v_justify`, with the values and defaults of `FootprintField`. The writer adds `left` or `right`, then `top` or `bottom`, to the `(justify …)` it already writes with `mirror` on a back layer; the reader projects them from `effects`. Rejected: `Text.mirrored`; the writer mirrors back-layer texts, and a read text keeps its own `effects` slot.

7. **`Dimension`: linear, and the text is a cache.** Kinds `aligned` and `orthogonal` (with `direction` `horizontal` or `vertical`, KiCad's `orientation` 0 and 1); `offset` is KiCad's `height`, whose sign the documentation gives with measurement 6; `units` `mm` or `in` (KiCad's units 2 and 0, with the suffix of `units_format 1`); `precision` from 0 to 4. The `style` child takes the values the demo boards write and 10.0.6 keeps (`arrow_length 1.27`, `text_position_mode 0`, `arrow_direction outward`, `extension_height 0.58642`, `extension_offset 0.5`, `keep_text_aligned yes`), with `thickness` from the model.
   - The writer's `gr_text` holds the measured length rounded half away from zero at `precision`, computed with integers (an integer square root for an aligned dimension), at the midpoint of the two points with angle 0. KiCad recomputes value, position and angle on load (measurement 6), so a difference in the last digit is never drawn.
   - The reader models `type`, `layer`, `pts`, `height` and `orientation`, and keeps `format`, `style` and the `gr_text` as projected opaque slots (units, precision, width, text size and thickness). The `gr_text`'s uuid belongs to the dimension and gives no duplicate warning. Other dimension types stay opaque root children (c0117 if its drawings need them).
   - Rejected: dimensions drawn as lines and texts, which do not follow the geometry. Rejected: computing KiCad's text position, a layout algorithm for a value KiCad overwrites.

8. **Script items are regenerated, as script copper is.** `boarditems.mark_items` gives every rule area, text, graphic and dimension of the built design the KiCad uuid `item_uuid(<id>)`: version 8, the 48-bit marker `fenitm` in `custom_a`, and the first 74 bits of the SHA-256 of `kicad-item:<id>`, as `copper_uuid` does. On a rebuild, `merge_items` drops every existing item that carries an item uuid. It is regenerated when the built design holds the uuid, with `kicad.board-item.regenerated` (info) when a modelled field differs, and stale otherwise, with `kicad.board-item.stale` (warning). Every other item stays; the built items follow the kept ones.
   - Rejected: the zone rule (the board wins unless the script locks the item). Four entities would need a lock field, a script change would not apply without it, and texts and drawings carry no name in the file by which an orphan is found.
   - Rejected: uuids without a marker, with which a stale drawing cannot be told from one drawn in KiCad.

9. **The copper check reads the geometry.** For each item and copper layer, `RuleSubject.areas` holds the names of the named rule areas on that layer whose filled outline touches the item's narrowed shape (`thick_touch`): copper, not centre lines, and only on the area's layers (measurement 3). `area` leaves match with `fnmatchcase`, so letter case counts; the other leaves keep folding.
   - Keep-outs: one `copper.keepout` error per item and keep-out, for a track or arc in an area forbidding tracks, a via in one forbidding vias and a pad in one forbidding pads, on a layer of the area. Fills are never reported (measurement 1); footprints are not copper (c0113).
   - An area whose outline is opaque (an arc in its `pts`) or refused by `Polygon` is counted as `copper.item-unsupported` of kind `rule-area`: its name matches nothing and its settings are not judged, and the stage drops to `UNVERIFIED` as for every lowering code.
   - Rejected: area rules left out of the check. The guard would then report clearance findings that a neck-down rule removes and pass those a high-voltage area adds.

10. **Requirements shared with other changes** (checked on `origin/dev` at `9aba2dff`, `openspec/changes/*/specs`, on 2026-10-07).
    - No open change on `dev` holds a delta of the four requirements this change modifies. c0071, c0068, c0069 and c0074 are archived, and two living texts moved since this proposal was written: "Closed selector grammar" (c0071: the `Reference` form of `ref` for courtyard rules, `selector_b` for `creepage`, `KIND_SELECTORS`, two scenarios) and "Clearance in force" (c0068: `zone_clearance`, the source `zone`, four scenarios). Both deltas were regenerated from the living text with this change's edits only: the `area` row and its rules, and `areas` on a subject.
    - "Closed selector grammar": this change, then c0104, which adds the leaf `diff_pair` and regenerates on the text this change leaves. The two add independent rows; if the order turns, this change regenerates.
    - "Modelled board content": c0101, then this change, then c0112. The delta here is the living text of `dev` with this change's edits; c0101 lands first (a prerequisite), so task 0.1 regenerates it on c0101's sentence about the stack-up. The edits touch different bullets.
    - "Identifier derivation": no open change on `dev` modifies it. c0096 modifies it on its branch (rows `hole` and `keepout`); both of its rows go (this design and c0102's, "Relation to c0096"), so one row holds the prefix `kpo`: `area:<area name>`. The table also gains the key `rule:named:<rule name>`, which c0071's `rule()` uses and the table lacked.
    - This change modifies neither "Copper items follow their nets" nor "Board content outside the design is kept" (c0102 modifies the second): "Board items declared in the script" takes its items out of both by name, as "Script copper in a merge" does.
    - c0100 lists a board's copper layers (`Design.copper_layers`); `rule_area(layers=None)` takes that list. c0102 owns `Edge.Cuts` and sends keep-outs around holes here.
    - c0074 (archived) drops stitch vias in an area that forbids vias. Script areas are in `Board.keepouts` before copper intents resolve, so its stitching sees them in the same build.
    - c0080 (agent track): if it is archived first, `rule_area` and `select.area` get one tested line on its guide page (task 8.2).

11. **The Altium build writes what it has a record for and names the rest.** Since c0085 a planned PCB document holds board items, so this proposal's first rule (one `altium.not-lowered` per kind, document or not) no longer describes `dev`. "Rule areas, board items and area rules in an Altium build" replaces it:
    - *Rule areas.* A script rule area is a `Keepout` of the model and is written by c0085's keep-out record with its restrictions. The record has no recorded key for a name, so the name is reported lost (`keepout/<id>`), as c0085 reports `no_footprints`. An area that forbids nothing has no record at all: that is what `keepout_problem` already answers for a keep-out without a restriction.
    - *Texts.* c0085's text record holds no justification. A text that is not centred is reported (`text/<id>`) and not written: writing it at the centred position would move it. Centred texts, the default, are written as before, so no sample changes.
    - *Dimensions.* A new kind of board item. It gets its place in the accounting (`KINDS` and `BOARD_KINDS` gain it), written 0, and each one is reported (`dimension/<id>`).
    - *Area rules.* `area` is a selector, not a rule kind, so c0084's table, where every kind has exactly one row, gains no row. The Altium scope grammar that the reader accepts has no area function, and `rulemap.lower` already answers `scope-unsupported` for any leaf other than `net` and `netclass`; the requirement states it for `area` and a test pins it. c0125 and c0130 (clearance scopes and matrix cells, open on `dev`) read Altium scopes into selectors and produce no `area` leaf.
    - *Copper guard.* c0088 runs `check_copper` on the document read back and lets only a short stop an Altium build; every other copper error is a warning there. `copper.keepout` follows that rule. The review of this proposal expected it to stop Altium builds; c0088's text says otherwise, and this change does not modify c0088's requirement.
    - *Texts inside footprints.* c0126 holds `Text` entities in `FootprintInstance.texts`. They gain `h_justify` and `v_justify` with the default `center`, which the canonical writer omits; c0126's KiCad projection and the Altium import leave them at the default, so no document and no fixture of c0126 changes.
    - Rejected: writing a justified text at its anchor as if centred. It is an approximation nobody would be told about.
    - Rejected: a new issue code for the Altium side. `altium.not-lowered` with `where` `<kind>/<id>` is c0085's way to name an item without a record.

12. **New model keys, and what an older release does with them.** `Keepout.name`, `Text.h_justify`, `Text.v_justify`, `Board.dimensions` and the selector op `area` are additive: the canonical writer omits defaults, and documents written before load unchanged. The other direction does not hold, as for every additive key (the convention c0126 set): release 0.2.x cannot read a model document that carries one of them, because its reader refuses an unknown key and its schema an unknown op. `docs/design-model.md` and the changelog say so (tasks 8.2 and 9.3), and the `board.json` that release 0.2.0 wrote, which c0126 commits as `tests/data/model/v0.2.0/blink_2layer.board.json`, must still load and serialise to its own bytes (task 2.1).

## Relation to c0096

c0096 (`constrained-board-placement`) is implemented on the branch `codex/c0096-constrained-placement` and is not on `dev` at `9aba2dff`. Its design records, in the table "Relation to c0103, c0113 and c0102", that its keep-out call conflicts with this change in API, ids and rebuild authority, and names `rule_area` for the common native object. The maintainer decided on 2026-10-07 (decision 2 of the v0.4 review): `rule_area` stands in place of `keepout()`, and c0096 adapts.

| c0096 has | what becomes of it |
|---|---|
| `Design.keepout(key, outline, layers=(), no_tracks=…, no_vias=…, no_pads=…, no_copper_pour=…, no_footprints=…) -> Keepout` | goes. `Design.rule_area(name, outline, *, layers=None, forbid=())` is the one call |
| `Design.keepouts`, the script's keep-outs by key | goes. `Design.rule_areas` holds the areas by name |

## Relation to c0097

c0097 (`copper-rule-explain`) is implemented on the branch `codex/c0097-copper-rule-explain` and is not on `dev`. It attaches the candidate rules, the two subjects and the governing value to a clearance finding and groups findings as intrinsic, inter-component and routed; its non-goals leave areas out, and it modifies no living requirement. Whichever of the two lands second adds three things, in its own tasks:

- the subject of an explanation shows `areas`, and a candidate row of a rule with an `area` leaf prints the leaf as `area <name>`, compared with letter case;
- a `copper.keepout` finding has no candidate rows: its explanation names the keep-out, the setting that forbids the item and the layer;
- a `copper.keepout` finding joins the grouping by its one item: `intrinsic` cannot apply (a keep-out is not part of a footprint), a pad gives `inter-component` and a track, arc or via `routed`.

Task 7.4 holds this for the case that c0097 is on `dev` first.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **An edit made in KiCad to a script item is undone.** Mitigation: `kicad.board-item.regenerated` names each one; an item the script does not declare is never touched, so a hand-drawn label stays.
- **Membership near an area's edge.** Measured at 50 µm on each side; the check uses an arc's narrowed shape, as for shorts, and exact contact is not measured. A copper item within a band of 1 µm of an edge may be judged differently from KiCad.
- **New `copper.keepout` errors stop builds.** KiCad's DRC already reports the same items as `items_not_allowed`; `--copper-check warn` writes anyway.
- **Hand-written area rules are now lifted and applied.** The written text does not change (names and clause order are kept by the lift and the lowering), but the copper check now applies such rules, where it reported `copper.rules-incomplete`.
- **The cache text of a dimension** may differ from KiCad's in its last digit; KiCad redraws it on load (measurement 6).
- **An area deleted in KiCad that a rule still names** stops the build with `build.area-unknown`; the hint says to declare the area with `rule_area()` or to remove the selector.
- **KiCad's DRC descriptions differ per major** (measurement 1). Every proof reads violation types and item uuids, never descriptions.
- **Altium builds gain infos.** Every named rule area gives one `altium.not-lowered` for its name, and an area for rules only one for the whole area. They state what the document does not hold; a design without the new calls gives none.
- **`copper.keepout` on Altium boards.** `fenolite check` of an imported board may now report copper in a keep-out that Altium's own check would also flag. No Altium oracle backs the predicate; the finding rests on the KiCad parity of `H-K-COPPER-AREA`, and the evidence of an Altium check stage does not rise for it.

## Migration Plan

- Additive for scripts: a design without the new calls builds the same bytes, and its `.fenolite/` cache only gains defaults.
- Release 0.2.x cannot read a model document that carries one of the new keys (`name` of a keep-out, `h_justify`, `v_justify`, `dimensions`) or a selector of the op `area`.
- If c0096 reached `dev` first, scripts written against its `Design.keepout(key, …)` change to `Design.rule_area(name, …, forbid=…)`; no release carried the old call.
- A board read with a named rule area, a justified text or a dimension now holds them in the model; its written text does not change, since every new child is a slot.
- Hand-written `intersectsArea` rules are lifted into the model, so `inspect`, the copper check and the build's guard see them; the rules text is written back the same.
- `check` and the copper guard may report `copper.keepout`; `CHANGELOG.md` says so, with `--copper-check warn` as the way to write anyway.
- Rollback: remove `area` from `SELECTOR_SUPPORT` (area rules stay opaque again), and the DSL calls; the reader's new fields fall back to slots.

## Open Questions

- **Regeneration or the zone rule for script items?** Default: regeneration (Decision 8). The maintainer may prefer that KiCad edits win unless locked, as for zones and fields.
- **Should `forbid` take `footprints` here, or in c0113?** Default: in c0113, which owns placement; the value is one entry of the same tuple.
- **Text and drawings on copper layers** (layer marks on each copper layer are a common fabrication request)? Default: refused until the copper check can shape glyphs.
- **Should `select.area()` refuse, at the call, a name the script does not declare?** Default: no; areas drawn in KiCad are valid targets, and the build checks after the merge.
- **A second selector for `enclosedByArea`?** Default: no, until a case needs it.
- **Precision above 4 decimals and inches with other suffixes for dimensions?** Default: no; nothing measured.
