## Why

The review of 2026-10-05 of the gaps to a complex board (milestone v0.4; `docs/roadmap.md`, "The complex board, c0100–c0120") found placement gaps that no change owns. Three of them are this change; all three are open at `origin/dev` `9aba2dff`:

- A script cannot keep a decoupling capacitor, a crystal or a gate driver near its pin, and nothing checks it. On the review's board of 600 parts, generated by a script authored for Fenolite and not committed, 113 capacitors sit a median 52.5 mm from their pin after the grid placer, 15.3 mm after the script's own placement; none within 3 mm.
- `place` and the build's guard ignore rule areas that forbid footprints, which KiCad's DRC reports, and the grid puts parts there.
- `place` and `check` only say "legal", while the grid variant of that board has 74 % more wire length and twice the congested cells.

The fourth gap of the review, part heights and height limits, is no longer here: the maintainer decided on 2026-10-07 to take it out into a change of its own, c0140-part-height-rules, written on c0099's body model so that a part's height has one source.

## What Changes

- **Proximity rules.** `design.near(key, parts, anchor, *, within)`: each part in `parts` (pads, parts or modules) keeps a pad within `within` of a pad of `anchor`.
- **Placement keep-outs.** `footprints` joins c0103's `rule_area(forbid=…)`; `place.keepout` follows KiCad's measured rule, and the grid avoids these areas. This predicate and this code are the ones Fenolite keeps: c0096's `place.constraint` no longer reports a part in such an area (design, "Relation to c0096").
- **Model.** `RuleSet.proximity`.
- **Measures.** Half-perimeter and spanning-tree wire length and a congestion grid, from pad positions.
- **Replies.** A default `check` stage, `placement.rules`, on KiCad projects and on Altium documents; `place` and `build` report rule findings as warnings, `place` the measures too.
- **Five issue codes**, each with its entry in `cli/data/explain.toml`: `place.keepout`, `place.keepout-no-courtyard`, `placement.too-far`, `placement.rule-unresolved`, `placement.rule-skipped`.

**Limits.** Distances join pad centres. Only built projects carry rules. Keep-outs judge front courtyards against areas on `F.Cu`, back ones against `B.Cu`, none against inner layers. KiCad ignores a part without a courtyard in a keep-out; Fenolite warns on its pad hull. Measures leave out zone nets, parts off the board and routed copper; congestion is an estimate; replies list five nets and five cells. `place` and `build` never refuse for a rule. The grid avoids areas by their boxes. `place` moves footprints of KiCad boards only; on Altium documents no stage judges a part in a keep-out.

Size: 5.5 design-days; cuts in the design.

## Non-goals

- Part heights and height limits: c0140-part-height-rules, after c0099.
- A placer that meets `near` rules: v0.5a in `docs/roadmap.md`, the annealing placer. Turning a `near` rule into an objective of c0096's bounded search: not in this change (design, Open Questions).
- A placement frame per module: no change has it; layout reuse per module is v0.5b in `docs/roadmap.md`.
- Minimum distances between parts: nowhere, because c0071's `courtyard_clearance` gives them.
- A gate driver's loop area: nowhere, because it needs the routed path; two `near` rules bound it.
- KiCad's own placement rule areas (multi-channel placement): nowhere; no change has them.
- Rules for a board without a script: nowhere, because neither KiCad's nor Altium's files can hold them.
- A placement guard in `build --target altium`, and `place` on Altium documents: nowhere in this change. The living "Placement legality in a build" says the guard does not run for that target; the rules of an Altium build are judged by `check`.
- Keep-outs sent to routers: c0107. Routed length: c0106.

## Impact

- New: `checks/placement.py` and a keep-out probe test. Changed: the model, the DSL, `placement`, `checks` (both pipelines), `cmd_place`, `cmd_build`, `backends/altium/lower.py`, `cli/data/explain.toml`, one schema, docs.
- `check` gains a default stage; `place` refuses a part in a keep-out unless `--force`.
- New model key `proximity` in `rules.json`, omitted when empty, so every output of 0.2.0 keeps its bytes; 0.2.x and 0.3.0 cannot read a `rules.json` that carries it.
- New source S-0680 (the proposal first reserved S-0390, which `dev` gave to another page).
- No rule kind: a `near` rule is a value object beside `RuleSet.rules`, so the KiCad lowering and the Altium rule table of c0084 are untouched.
