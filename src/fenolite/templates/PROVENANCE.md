# Provenance of `fenolite.templates`

Every figure the shipped sheet examples use comes from a public source listed in
`docs/evidence/sources.md`, or is a Fenolite choice recorded per number in the example's
`[provenance.values]` table. The figures are written in Fenolite's own words in
`docs/formats/sheets.md`; no text or figure of a standard is reproduced, and no KiCad sheet (its
default sheet or the kicad-templates sheets) is read.
