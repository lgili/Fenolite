Geometry filters · millimetres

Spacing is the minimum distance between distinct numbered-pad centres, rounded down by less than 1 nm; it is not manufacturer pitch. Width/height bounds apply to every numbered pad in its local axes. Missing spacing cannot match an active spacing filter.

Pin-to-pad map
